# Mission: Pass the Israel Supermarket / Manufacturing Interview in English

## Why
You are preparing for a one-time, high-stakes job interview conducted in English by Israeli representatives and SLBFE officers for a supermarket, retail logistics, or manufacturing position in Israel (B1 work visa, G2G program). Passing this interview unlocks a 5-year international work opportunity, a salary in New Israeli Shekels, and a meaningful economic upgrade for your family. Failing it means months — possibly years — of waiting for another chance.

## Success looks like
- Deliver a fluent, under-one-minute self-introduction with correct pronunciation of all numbers, names, places, and key English phrases.
- Answer 30+ interview questions (supermarket, manufacturing, Israel, adaptability, longevity) with clear, simple SVO sentences.
- Pass the Ben Gurion airport immigration drill without triggering suspicion.
- Speak with controlled pacing, eye contact (camera), and upright posture throughout a recorded interview.
- Hold 2,000–3,000 high-frequency English words and industry-specific vocabulary at active recall level.

## Constraints
- Time: interview date is approaching — sessions must be 30–45 minutes each, not 2 hours.
- Language: Sinhala is your native language; English explanations should be paired with short Sinhala clarifications only when necessary — primary input is in English.
- Method: Comprehensible Input throughout — meaning before grammar, recycling, mnemonics, micro-learning (≤5-min chunks), spaced repetition (1 hr / 24 hr / 1 week / 1 month reviews).
- Pronunciation matters: numbers (15 vs 50, 13 vs 30) are a documented fail-trigger.
- The 8/5-step self-introduction must stay under 60 seconds — length is a documented fail-trigger.

## Out of scope (for this course)
- General grammar textbooks (verb tenses theory, parts of speech).
- Academic English (IELTS, TOEFL writing tasks).
- Hebrew language.
- Israeli permanent-residency or citizenship procedures.
