## Pedagogical references
- **Stephen Krashen** — *The Input Hypothesis* and *i+1* level (foundational Comprehensible Input theory).
- **Dunlosky et al. (2013)** — *Improving Students' Learning with Effective Learning Techniques* — retrieval practice vs. re-reading.
- **Karpicke & Blunt (2011)** — Retrieval-based learning outperforms passive concept mapping.
- **Ariga & Lleras (2011, *Cognition*)** — Brief mental deactivations preserve vigilance (basis for the strategic-break method).
