Lesson 0010 - Visa, Loyalty, Return, And Future Plans

Main target: Show legal compliance, loyalty to the sponsor employer, and a clear return plan.

Remember First

B1 visa answer: legal B1 work visa, sponsor employer only, no unauthorized side job, respect the visa period.

PLAN = Protect legal status - Loyal service - Accumulate savings and skills - New business in Sri Lanka.

Use the current official visa information supplied by SLBFE or your employer. The interview guide states a maximum of 5 years and 3 months; verify this before the interview.

Interview Questions And Model Answers

1. “Under what visa will you work?”

“I will work under a legal B1 work visa. I will work only for my sponsor employer and follow all visa conditions.”

2. “Can you work for another employer or do a side job?”

“No, Sir. I will work only for my sponsor employer. I will not do unauthorized part-time work.”

3. “Can you stay with the same company for the full contract?”

“Yes, Sir. I am fully committed to loyal, long-term service and completing my contract.”

4. “What will you do when your visa expires?”

“I will follow immigration law and return to Sri Lanka. I will not overstay my visa.”

5. “What are your future plans?”

“I plan to use my savings and international retail experience to start or expand a retail business in Sri Lanka.”

6. “What is your ambition?”

“I want to become a skilled retail professional and eventually develop my own business in Sri Lanka.”

Comprehensible Input: The Five-Year Plan

Dilan works legally for his sponsor employer. He follows the contract and does not take a second job. He saves money and learns modern retail skills. When the legal work period ends, he returns to Sri Lanka and uses his savings and experience for a retail business.

Read it twice. Then retell it from legal work - loyal service - savings - return - business.

Pronunciation And Vocabulary

  • visa: VEE-zuh
  • sponsor: SPON-ser
  • legal: LEE-gul
  • compliance: kum-PLY-uhns
  • overstay: OH-ver-stay

Retrieval Check

Who may you work for?Only the sponsor employer, according to the legal visa conditions.
What will you do when the visa expires?Follow the law and return to Sri Lanka.
What is the PLAN sequence?Protect legal status, Loyal service, Accumulate savings and skills, New business in Sri Lanka.

Practice

  • [ ] Answer the six questions without saying “forever” or “I don’t know.”
  • [ ] State the visa rule in two sentences.
  • [ ] State your return plan in two sentences.
  • [ ] Make sure the future plan is positive but truthful.

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Recite B1 rules and PLAN.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Answer visa, loyalty, return, and ambition questions.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Mix compliance with adaptability questions.
  • [ ] Before interview: Verify current legal wording with official documents.

Progress Criterion

Move on when you can explain legal work, loyalty, return, and future plans without suggesting illegal work or permanent overstaying.
