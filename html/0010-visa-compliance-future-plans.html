Lesson 0010 — Visa Regulations, Contract Longevity & Future Plans

Mission link: Recruiters want to ensure that candidates will work faithfully for the full 5-year contract, respect Israeli immigration laws, and return to Sri Lanka legally when the visa expires. If you mention illegal part-time work, show uncertainty about staying 5 years, or fail to state a clear future plan, your visa sponsorship will be canceled immediately. This lesson teaches you how to articulate 100% legal compliance and long-term ambition.

Strict Comprehensible Input mode: Follow Dilan across three narrative scenes: understanding his B1 visa contract, demonstrating loyalty on the job, and sharing his 5-year entrepreneurial vision upon returning to Sri Lanka. Comprehension precedes gentle speaking drills. Grammar rules are in the Optional Monitor Appendix.

Personal details used (real): Dilan Harshana, age 28, from Pahalagoda, Tangalle, single, disciplined, planning to save his earnings in New Israeli Shekels to build his own modern retail business back in Tangalle.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson — through comprehensible input, not mechanical memorization — you will be able to:
1. Explain the rules of the B1 work visa (5 years 3 months maximum, single sponsor employer, no illegal side jobs).
2. State 100% commitment to completing the full 5-year contract with high loyalty.
3. Articulate your return plan and future business ambition upon completing your legal stay in Israel.
4. Demonstrate understanding of legal immigration compliance (zero overstaying).
5. Hold 2 core compliance memory formulas: B.1.-V.I.S.A. and P.L.A.N.

Estimated time: 40 minutes, split into 5 micro-blocks of ~8 min each.
Comprehensible Input level: i + 1 — legal and career concepts expressed in crystal-clear, persuasive SVO English.

The Big Idea (read in English — visualize the interview dialogue)

The Israeli panel looks intently at Dilan and asks two critical closing questions:
"Dilan, can you work for the same company for the full 5 years, and what will you do when your visa expires?"

Dilan sits straight, looks into the camera, and answers with sincere conviction:
"Yes, Sir. I am 100% committed to working loyally for my sponsor company for the full 5 years. When my visa expires, I will strictly follow immigration laws and return to Sri Lanka. I plan to use my savings and international retail experience to start my own supermarket business in my hometown."

The panel writes: "Trustworthy, compliant, high long-term retention probability. PASSED."

Recruiters love candidates who have a clear, positive vision for their money and career.

Micro-Block 1 — Pure Immersion: The B1 Visa Rules (8 min)

CI principle applied: Pre-input immersion through legal clarity ($i+1$).

1A. Scene 1 — The B1 Work Visa Contract (4 min)

Read this story slowly. Understand the legal framework:

Dilan reviews the official Government-to-Government (G2G) bilateral agreement between Sri Lanka and Israel.
Under this program, he will be issued a B1 Work Visa.

Dilan understands the four strict legal rules:
1. Validity: The B1 visa is valid for a maximum duration of 5 years and 3 months.
2. Single Employer: He is legally bound to work only for his allocated sponsor employer.
3. No Side Jobs: Working part-time or moonlight jobs for another company is strictly illegal and causes immediate deportation.
4. Mandatory Return: When the contract concludes, the worker must return home to Sri Lanka.

Dilan is law-abiding and disciplined. He respects these regulations 100%.

1B. The B.1.-V.I.S.A. Legal memory formula (4 min)

Letter Rule Professional Interview Sentence
B Bound to 1 Sponsor "I will work only for my allocated sponsor company."
V Valid for 5 Years "My visa is valid for up to 5 years and 3 months."
I Illegal Side Jobs Banned "I will never engage in unauthorized part-time work."
S Save Capital "I will save my earnings systematically in Shekels."
A Always Return Legally "I will return to Sri Lanka when my visa expires."

Micro-Block 2 — Contract Longevity & Company Loyalty (8 min)

CI principle applied: Repetition with variation through career stability scenarios.

2A. Scene 2 — The 5-Year Journey (5 min)

It costs Israeli employers time and money to recruit, fly, and train foreign workers. If a worker leaves early or complains constantly, the company suffers losses.

Israeli recruiters test candidates for job stability:
- "Will you quit after one year?" → "No, Sir. I am 100% committed to completing my 5-year contract."
- "Can you stay loyal to one company?" → "Yes, Sir. I provide stable, loyal, and dedicated service."

Dilan explains that having a stable 5-year tenure allows him to master advanced supermarket operations and maximize his savings for his family.

Micro-Block 3 — The Future Plan: Entrepreneurship in Sri Lanka (8 min)

CI principle applied: Positive personal ambition in clear narrative context.

3A. Scene 3 — Dilan's Tangalle Retail Vision (5 min)

When the interview board asks: "What is your goal / ambition after 5 years?"
Dilan does not say: "I don't know" or "I want to stay in Israel forever."

Instead, Dilan shares his concrete, inspiring plan:
- He will work hard in Israel for 5 years and save his salary in New Israeli Shekels.
- He will learn modern Israeli supermarket inventory methods, automated shelving, and customer service standards.
- When he returns to Sri Lanka, he will use his capital and experience to open and expand his own modern retail grocery supermarket in Tangalle.

This shows the interview panel that Dilan has a purpose, self-discipline, and strong character.

Micro-Block 5 — Output & Self-Verification Drills (8 min)

CI principle applied: Gradual output release: Whisper drill → Cloze comprehension → Journal recording.

5A. The 3-Sentence Whisper Drill (3 min)

Whisper these 3 statements 5 times each with steady confidence:
1. "I will work under a B1 visa and respect all legal regulations."
2. "I am 100% committed to completing my 5-year contract loyally."
3. "I will return to Sri Lanka to start my own business with my savings."

5B. Cloze Comprehension Check (3 min)

Fill in the blanks with the correct compliance terms:

  1. The maximum legal duration of the B1 work visa is _ years and _ months.
    (Choices: 5 / 3)
  2. Working for a second company or doing unauthorized side jobs is strictly __.
    (Choices: illegal / encouraged)
  3. When the contract concludes, the worker must __ to Sri Lanka.
    (Choices: return / hide)
Click for Answer Key 1. 5 / 3 2. illegal 3. return

5C. 3-Words-a-Day Journal Entries (2 min)

Write these 3 words in your vocabulary notebook:
1. Compliance /kəmˈplaɪəns/ (Noun) = Obeying laws, rules, and regulations. (Ex: "I maintain 100% legal compliance with my visa.")
2. Longevity /lɒnˈdʒɛvɪti/ (Noun) = Long duration of service in a job. (Ex: "Employers value contract longevity.")
3. Entrepreneur /ˌɒntrəprəˈnɜːr/ (Noun) = A person who starts their own business. (Ex: "I plan to become a retail entrepreneur.")

Optional Monitor Appendix (For Grammar Enthusiasts Only)

Note: Language acquisition happens naturally through understanding messages. Grammar rules below are for reference only.

  • Future Intentions with "Plan to" & "Will":
  • Use "I plan to [verb]" for long-term career goals: "I plan to start a business."
  • Use "I will [verb]" for firm legal commitments: "I will return to Sri Lanka."
  • Avoid Overstaying Language: Never use words like "permanently", "forever", or "settle down" when discussing Israel. Always emphasize "legal contract period".

භාවිතා වන CI මූලධර්මය (CI principle applied): මෘදු ප්‍රකාශන පියවර: Whisper කිරීම → Cloze අවබෝධය → Vocabulary සටහන් පොත.

5A. The 3-Sentence Whisper Drill (විනාඩි 3)

පහත ප්‍රකාශ 3 එකක් 5 වතාවක් බැගින් ස්ථිර හඬින් whisper කරන්න:
1. "I will work under a B1 visa and respect all legal regulations."
2. "I am 100% committed to completing my 5-year contract loyally."
3. "I will return to Sri Lanka to start my own business with my savings."

5B. Cloze Comprehension Check — හිස්තැන් පුරවන්න (විනාඩි 3)

නිවැරදි නීතිමය වචන යොදා හිස්තැන් පුරවන්න:

  1. The maximum legal duration of the B1 work visa is _ years and _ months.
    (තෝරාගැනීම්: 5 / 3)
  2. Working for a second company or doing unauthorized side jobs is strictly __.
    (තෝරාගැනීම්: illegal / encouraged)
  3. When the contract concludes, the worker must __ to Sri Lanka.
    (තෝරාගැනීම්: return / hide)
නිවැරදි පිළිතුරු (Answer Key) 1. 5 / 3 2. illegal 3. return

5C. 3-Words-a-Day Journal Entries (විනාඩි 2)

ඔබගේ සටහන් පොතට මෙම වචන 3 එකතු කරන්න:
1. Compliance /kəmˈplaɪəns/ (Noun) = නීති රීති සහ රෙගුලාසිවලට අනුකූල වීම. (Ex: "I maintain 100% legal compliance with my visa.")
2. Longevity /lɒnˈdʒɛvɪti/ (Noun) = රැකියාවක දිගු කාලයක් සේවය කිරීමේ හැකියාව. (Ex: "Employers value contract longevity.")
3. Entrepreneur /ˌɒntrəprəˈnɜːr/ (Noun) = තමන්ගේම ව්‍යාපාරයක් අලුතින් අරඹන ව්‍යවසායකයා. (Ex: "I plan to become a retail entrepreneur.")
