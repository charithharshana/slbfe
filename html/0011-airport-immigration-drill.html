Lesson 0011 - Airport Immigration Answers

Main target: Give short, factual answers at Ben Gurion Airport and show the correct documents.

Remember First

PASS = Purpose - Address - Stay - Screen declaration.

Keep your passport, B1 visa, employment letter, accommodation address, and G2G documents ready. Answer only the question asked.

The Four Questions To Memorize

1. “What is the purpose of your visit?”

“I am here to work under a legal government B1 visa. Here is my employment letter.”

Do not say “I am here for fun” or “I want to stay forever.”

2. “Where will you stay?”

“I will stay at the employee accommodation provided by my company. Here is the official address.”

3. “How long will you stay?”

“I will stay for the duration of my legal work visa.”

4. “Do you have anything to declare?”

“No, Sir. I have nothing to declare.”

If you carry permitted packaged food, say exactly what it is and declare it. Never hide fresh fruit, plants, raw vegetables, or other restricted items.

Comprehensible Input: At The Border

Dilan reaches the border counter with his passport and work documents. The officer asks four questions. Dilan answers about legal work, company accommodation, the visa period, and customs. His answers are short and match his papers. He stays calm and does not make jokes.

Read the story twice. Then cover it and recall work - address - visa - declaration.

Retrieval Check

What is the purpose of the visit?Legal work under a government B1 visa.
What should you show for accommodation?The official company accommodation address or employment letter.
What should you do with restricted food or agricultural items?Do not hide them; follow customs rules and declare permitted items accurately.

Practice

  • [ ] Role-play all four questions standing up.
  • [ ] Hand over your passport and employment letter smoothly.
  • [ ] Replace “forever” with “for the duration of my legal work visa.”
  • [ ] Answer in one or two factual sentences.

Pronunciation And Vocabulary

  • immigration: im-i-GRAY-shun
  • accommodation: uh-kom-uh-DAY-shun
  • declaration: dek-luh-RAY-shun
  • duration: dyoo-RAY-shun

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Say the four answers.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Role-play the border officer dialogue.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Practise with the real document folder.
  • [ ] Before travel: Check visa, passport, employment letter, address, and customs requirements.

Progress Criterion

Move on when you can answer all four questions without vague wording, jokes, or document searching.

ප්‍රධාන ඉලක්කය: Ben Gurion Airport (ගුවන් තොටුපළ) ආගමන විගමන නිලධාරීන් හමුවේ කෙටි, සත්‍ය පිළිතුරු දීම සහ අදාළ ලේඛන පෙන්වීම.

අකුර පියවර අර්ථය

2. The 4 Essential Airport Q&A

  1. Purpose of Visit:
    - Q: "What is the purpose of your visit?"
    - A: "I am here to work under a legal government B1 visa. Here is my employment letter."

  2. Accommodation Address:
    - Q: "Where will you stay?"
    - A: "I will stay at the employee accommodation provided by my company. Here is the official address."

  3. Duration of Stay:
    - Q: "How long will you stay?"
    - A: "I will stay for the duration of my legal work visa."

  4. Customs Declaration:
    - Q: "Do you have anything to declare?"
    - A: "No, Sir. I have nothing to declare." (තහනම්/අස්වාභාවික ද්‍රව්‍ය ගෙන නොයන්න; අවසර ලත් ආහාර තිබේ නම් සත්‍ය ප්‍රකාශ කරන්න)

දෛනික වැදගත් වචන 3 (3 Key Words)

  • Immigration (ආගමන විගමනය — Pronounced: "im-i-GRAY-shun") — "Pass through airport immigration."
  • Accommodation (නවාතැන් ලිපිනය — Pronounced: "uh-kom-uh-DAY-shun") — "Here is my company accommodation address."
  • Duration (කාල සීමාව — Pronounced: "dyoo-RAY-shun") — "For the duration of my work visa."
