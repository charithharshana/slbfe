Lesson 0001 - Your 60-Second Introduction

Main target: Answer “Tell me about yourself” clearly in less than one minute.
This is the most important answer in the course. Use only facts that match your documents and employer verification.

Interview Card

Question: “Please tell me about yourself.”

Model answer:

“Good morning, Sir. Thank you for giving me this opportunity. My name is Dilan Harshana. I am twenty-eight years old, not married, and I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka. I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work. I trained at Keells Supermarket for three months and worked at a retail supermarket in Tangalle for more than five years. My duties included helping customers, arranging stock, and keeping the supermarket clean and organized. I have also completed a forklift operator course and trained at David Pieris Motors Company. I am hardworking, honest, reliable, and physically fit. I want to work in Israel to gain international experience and develop my skills. That is all about me. Thank you, Sir.”

Use your real details only. If a document gives a different job title, date, salary, or period, use the document’s exact information.

Remember The Shape

PESMS = Profile - Experience - Strengths - Motivation - Sign-off.

  • Profile: name, age, marital status, hometown
  • Experience: supermarket work, Keells training, retail duties, forklift course
  • Strengths: hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit
  • Motivation: international experience and skill development
  • Sign-off: “That is all about me. Thank you, Sir.”

Think of five shelves. Put one group of information on each shelf. Do not tell your whole life story.

Comprehensible Input Routine

  1. Understand: Read the model answer twice. Picture Dilan entering the interview room and speaking to the panel.
  2. Notice: Read it again and point to the five PESMS shelves. Do not translate every word.
  3. Retrieve: Hide the answer. Say the five shelves and one fact for each.
  4. Output last: Read once, whisper once, then speak once. Record only when you feel calm.

Short Input Story

Dilan enters the room. He greets the panel. First, he says who he is and where he is from. Next, he explains his supermarket experience and duties. Then, he describes his strengths and training. He explains why he wants to work in Israel. Finally, he closes politely. His answer is short, clear, and professional.

Essential Follow-Up Questions

Interviewer asks Safe answer
“What is your name?” “My name is Dilan Harshana.”
“How old are you?” “I am twenty-eight years old.”
“Where are you from?” “I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka.”
“What is your occupation?” “I work in supermarket and retail operations.”
“How many years of experience do you have?” “I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work.”
“What are your duties?” “I help customers, arrange stock, and keep the supermarket clean and organized.”
“Why do you want this job?” “I want to gain international experience and develop my skills.”
“Why should we hire you?” “I am hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit, and ready to learn.”

Pronunciation And Delivery

  • Speak slowly. Pause after each PESMS section.
  • Say twenty-eight, not “twenty eight” too quickly.
  • Stress the important words: five years, retail, forklift, reliable, Israel.
  • Look at the camera, sit upright, and keep a small natural smile.
  • Target: 45-60 seconds, not longer.

Retrieval Check

What does PESMS mean?Profile, Experience, Strengths, Motivation, Sign-off.
What are Dilan’s three main work details?More than five years in supermarket and retail work, three months of training at Keells, and a forklift operator course.
What must you never invent?Any job title, duty, salary, date, certificate, or experience that cannot be verified.

Practice

  1. Read the model answer and underline the five PESMS sections.
  2. Close the page and write five cue words: Profile / Experience / Strengths / Motivation / Sign-off.
  3. Speak from the cue words, not from a full script.
  4. Time yourself. Stop before 60 seconds.

Three Useful Words

  • reliable - someone people can trust to do the work
  • responsibility - a duty or task you must do
  • opportunity - a good chance to do something

Use them in context: “I am reliable.” “My responsibilities included arranging stock.” “Thank you for this opportunity.”

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Say PESMS and the five facts without reading.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Speak the introduction twice and check the time.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Answer the eight follow-up questions in random order.
  • [ ] After 1 month / before interview: Record the full introduction with camera eye contact.

Progress Criterion

Move on when you can give the introduction in 45-60 seconds, with accurate personal facts, without reading every sentence.

Remember The Shape (ආකෘතිය මතක තබා ගන්න)

PESMS = Profile - Experience - Strengths - Motivation - Sign-off.

  • Profile: name, age, marital status, hometown (නම, වයස, විවාහක තත්ත්වය, ගම)
  • Experience: supermarket work, Keells training, retail duties, forklift course (supermarket රැකියාව, Keells පුහුණුව, retail රාජකාරි, forklift පාඨමාලාව)
  • Strengths: hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit (කැපවීමෙන් වැඩ කරන, අවංක, විශ්වාසවන්ත, කායිකව යෝග්‍ය)
  • Motivation: international experience and skill development (ජාත්‍යන්තර පළපුරුද්ද සහ කුසලතා වර්ධනය)
  • Sign-off: “That is all about me. Thank you, Sir.” (ගෞරවනීය නිමාව)

තට්ටු පහක shelf එකක් ගැන සිතන්න. සෑම shelf එකකම එක තොරතුරු කාණ්ඩයක් තබන්න. ඔබේ මුළු ජීවිත කතාවම පැවසීමට යන්න එපා.

Comprehensible Input Routine (අවබෝධාත්මක Input පියවර)

  1. Understand (වටහා ගන්න): Model answer එක දෙපාරක් කියවන්න. ඩිලාන් interview room එකට ඇතුළු වී panel එකට කතා කරන ආකාරය සිතින් මවාගන්න (picture it).
  2. Notice (නිරීක්ෂණය කරන්න): නැවත කියවා PESMS shelves පහ වෙත අවධානය යොමු කරන්න. සෑම වචනයක්ම පරිවර්තනය කිරීමට යන්න එපා.
  3. Retrieve (මතකයෙන් ගන්න): Answer එක වසා තබන්න. Shelves පහ සහ ඒ එකකට අදාළ එක් සත්‍ය තොරතුර බැගින් මතකයෙන් පවසන්න.
  4. Output last (අවසානයේ කතා කරන්න): එක පාරක් හිතින් කියවන්න, එක පාරක් හෙමින් මිමිණීමට (whisper) කරන්න, ඊට පසු ශබ්ද නඟා පවසන්න. සන්සුන් බවක් දැනෙන විට පමණක් record කරන්න.

Lesson 0001 — Your 60-Second Introduction (තත්පර 60 කෙටි මතක පාඩම)

ප්‍රධාන ඉලක්කය: මිනිත්තුවකට (තත්පර 45-60) වඩා අඩු කාලයකින් "Tell me about yourself" ප්‍රශ්නයට පැහැදිලිව, සත්‍ය තොරතුරු පමණක් ඇතුළත් කර පිළිතුරු දීම.

core Structure: PESMS සූත්‍රය (පියවර 5)

ඔබේ self-introduction එක තට්ටු 5ක shelf එකක් මෙන් පහත පිළිවෙලට සැකසේ:

අකුර පියවර අර්ථය උදාහරණ වාක්‍යය
P Profile නම, වයස, විවාහක තත්ත්වය, ගම "Good morning, Sir. Thank you for giving me this opportunity. My name is Dilan Harshana. I am twenty-eight years old, not married, and I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka."
E Experience පළපුරුද්ද, පුහුණුව සහ රාජකාරි "I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work. I trained at Keells Supermarket for three months and worked at a retail supermarket in Tangalle for more than five years. My duties included helping customers, arranging stock, and keeping the supermarket clean and organized. I have also completed a forklift operator course and trained at David Pieris Motors Company."
S Strengths පෞද්ගලික ගුණාංග "I am hardworking, honest, reliable, and physically fit."
M Motivation රැකියාවට ඇති අරමුණ "I want to work in Israel to gain international experience and develop my skills."
S Sign-off ගෞරවනීය අවසානය "That is all about me. Thank you, Sir."

අතේ ඇඟිලි 5 මතක ක්‍රමය (5-Finger Memory Trick)

Interview එකේදී මතකය අවදි කරගැනීමට ඇඟිලි 5 මවාගන්න:
1. සුළැඟිල්ල (Pinky): Profile (මම කවුද)
2. වෙදැඟිල්ල (Ring): Experience (මගේ පළපුරුද්ද)
3. මැදඟිල්ල (Middle): Strengths (මගේ ශක්තීන්)
4. දබරැඟිල්ල (Index): Motivation (මගේ අරමුණ)
5. මාපටැඟිල්ල (Thumb): Sign-off (අවසාන ස්තූතිය)

රාජකාරි 3 මතක යතුර: H-A-C (Hac) සූත්‍රය

Supermarket රාජකාරි පැහැදිලි කිරීමට H-A-C මතක තබා ගන්න:
- H - Help: "Helping customers" (පාරිභෝගිකයින්ට සහාය වීම)
- A - Arrange: "Arranging stock" (තොග පිළියෙල කිරීම)
- C - Clean: "Keeping clean and organized" (පිරිසිදුව හා පිළිවෙළට තබා ගැනීම)

අංක උච්චාරණ රහස: Teen vs Ty Rule

  • -Teen (-ටීන්): ළමයෙකු මෙන් ඉහළට ඇදෙන දිගු ශබ්දය (13-19) — e.g., twenty-eight, fifteen (15)
  • -Ty (-ටී): වැඩිහිටියෙකු මෙන් කෙටි බර ශබ්දය (30, 40, 50) — e.g., fifty (50)
  • Delivery Tip: සෑම PESMS කොටසකටම පසුව කුඩා pause එකක් ගන්න. Key words stress කරන්න: five years, retail, forklift, reliable, Israel.

අත්‍යවශ්‍ය අනුගමන ප්‍රශ්න (Essential Follow-Up Q&A)

Interviewer asks Safe Answer
“What is your name?” “My name is Dilan Harshana.”
“How old are you?” “I am twenty-eight years old.”
“Where are you from?” “I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka.”
“What is your occupation?” “I work in supermarket and retail operations.”
“How many years of experience do you have?” “I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work.”
“What are your duties?” “I help customers, arrange stock, and keep the supermarket clean and organized.”
“Why do you want this job?” “I want to gain international experience and develop my skills.”
“Why should we hire you?” “I am hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit, and ready to learn.”

දෛනික වැදගත් වචන 3 (3 Key Words)

  • Reliable (විශ්වාසදායක) — "I am reliable."
  • Responsibility (වගකීම / රාජකාරිය) — "My responsibilities included arranging stock."
  • Opportunity (අවස්ථාව) — "Thank you for this opportunity."

1-1-1-1 මතක සමාලෝචනය (Spaced Review Routine)

  • පැයක් ඇතුළත: PESMS සහ කරුණු 5 මතකයෙන් පවසන්න.
  • දිනකට පසු: Introduction එක 2 පාරක් කියා time කරගන්න (තත්පර 45-60).
  • සතියකට පසු: Follow-up questions 8ට පිළිවෙලක් නැතිව පිළිතුරු දෙන්න.
  • මසකට පසු / Interview එකට පෙර: Camera එක ඉදිරියේ full introduction එක record කරන්න.
