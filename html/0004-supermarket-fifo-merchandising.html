Lesson 0004 - Supermarket Questions: FIFO, Safety, And Customers

Main target: Answer the practical supermarket questions that prove you understand retail work.

Remember First

FIFO = First-In, First-Out: old stock goes to the front; new stock goes to the back.

SPILL = Secure - Place sign - Isolate glass - Liquid mop - Leave clean.

HEAT = Hear - Empathize - Apologize - Take action.

Interview Questions And Model Answers

1. “What is FIFO, and how do you use it?”

“FIFO stands for First-In, First-Out. I check the expiration dates. I put new products at the back and move older products to the front. This keeps products fresh and reduces waste.”

2. “What do you do if a bottle breaks in a busy aisle?”

“Customer safety is my first priority. I secure the area and place a warning sign. I wear gloves and remove the broken glass safely. Then I mop the liquid with the correct chemical. I remove the sign only when the floor is dry.”

3. “What do you do if a display shelf is empty?”

“I replenish the shelf immediately. I bring stock from the back storeroom. If the item is out of stock, I inform my supervisor.”

4. “How do you help a customer when you cannot understand the language?”

“I stay calm and polite. I try to understand what the customer needs. If I cannot help, I find a Hebrew-speaking supervisor or manager.”

5. “How do you handle a customer complaint?”

“I listen without interrupting. I show empathy and apologize for the inconvenience. Then I take action or call my supervisor.”

6. “Why is chemical safety important?”

“The correct chemical prevents accidents and keeps the store safe. I wear protective gloves and follow the instructions for each chemical.”

7. “How do you work during a long, repetitive shift?”

“I stay focused and keep a steady pace. I work systematically so the shelves stay full, organized, and accurate.”

8. “What do you do if you see theft?”

“I report it to my supervisor. I do not confront the person myself.”

9. “What do you do if you make a mistake?”

“I report it immediately, fix it quickly, and learn from it.”

Comprehensible Input: A Morning On The Floor

New milk arrives. Dilan checks the dates. He pulls older milk to the front and places new milk at the back. Later, a bottle breaks. He secures the aisle, puts up a warning sign, removes the glass with gloves, and mops the floor. A customer needs help. Dilan listens, stays polite, and finds a supervisor when necessary.

Read the scene three times: full scene, short phrases, then from memory using FIFO - SPILL - HEAT.

Key Vocabulary And Pronunciation

  • aisle: “айл”; the s is silent
  • replenish: ri-PLEN-ish; fill again
  • expiration date: the date after which food may not be safe
  • hazard: danger
  • supervisor: manager responsible for the work

Retrieval Check

Where does old stock go?To the front.
What is the first action after a spill?Secure the area and protect customers.
What should you do when a customer’s language is difficult?Stay calm, try to understand, and find a supervisor if necessary.

Practice

  • [ ] Explain FIFO in four sentences.
  • [ ] Explain a spill using all five SPILL steps.
  • [ ] Answer one customer complaint using HEAT.
  • [ ] Keep each answer under 30 seconds.

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Say FIFO and SPILL from memory.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Answer questions 1-5 in random order.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Answer one technical question on a recording.
  • [ ] Before interview: Mix FIFO, spill, customer, chemical, shelf, theft, and mistake questions.

Progress Criterion

Move on when you can explain FIFO and a spill in order, using calm short sentences.

Lesson 0004 — Supermarket Questions: FIFO, Safety, And Customers (සුපර්මාර්කට් ප්‍රශ්න)

ප්‍රධාන ඉලක්කය: Retail / Supermarket ක්ෂේත්‍රයේ ප්‍රායෝගික ප්‍රශ්න (Stock, Safety, Customer Service) සඳහා සාර්ථකව පිළිතුරු දීම.

1. Stock Rotation: FIFO සූත්‍රය

  • FIFO = First-In, First-Out
  • Old stock: Front (පැරණි තොග ඉදිරියට)
  • New stock: Back (අලුත් තොග පිටුපසට)
  • Q: "What is FIFO, and how do you use it?"
  • A: "FIFO stands for First-In, First-Out. I check the expiration dates. I put new products at the back and move older products to the front. This keeps products fresh and reduces waste."

2. Accident & Spill Handling: SPILL සූත්‍රය

වීදුරු බෝතලයක් / දියරයක් බිම වැටුණහොත් ක්‍රියාත්මක වන පියවර 5:

අකුර පියවර ක්‍රියාව
S Secure Secure the area (ප්‍රදේශය ආරක්ෂිත කර පාරිභෝගිකයින් වළක්වන්න)
P Place sign Place a warning sign ("Caution Wet Floor")
I Isolate glass Wear gloves & isolate broken glass safely
L Liquid mop Mop the liquid with correct chemicals
L Leave clean Remove warning sign only when the floor is completely dry
  • Q: "What do you do if a bottle breaks in a busy aisle?"
  • A: "Customer safety is my first priority. I secure the area and place a warning sign. I wear gloves and remove the broken glass safely. Then I mop the liquid with the correct chemical. I remove the sign only when the floor is dry."

3. Customer Service & Complaints: HEAT සූත්‍රය

පාරිභෝගික පැමිණිලි විසඳන පියවර 4:
- H - Hear: Listen without interrupting (නොකඩා සවන්දෙන්න)
- E - Empathize: Show empathy (අනුකම්පාව දක්වන්න)
- A - Apologize: Apologize for the inconvenience (සමාව ඉල්ලන්න)
- T - Take action: Take action or call supervisor (ක්‍රියාමාර්ග ගන්න හෝ superior ට පවසන්න)

  • Q: "How do you help a customer when you cannot understand the language?"
  • A: "I stay calm and polite. I try to understand what the customer needs. If I cannot help, I find a Hebrew-speaking supervisor or manager."

4. වෙනත් අත්‍යවශ්‍ය Supermarket Q&A

  1. Empty Display Shelf: "I replenish the shelf immediately from the back storeroom. If out of stock, I inform my supervisor."
  2. Chemical Safety: "The correct chemical prevents accidents. I wear protective gloves and follow chemical instructions."
  3. Long Shifts: "I stay focused and keep a steady pace so shelves stay full, organized, and accurate."
  4. Theft: "I report it to my supervisor immediately. I do not confront the person myself."
  5. Mistakes: "I report it immediately, fix it quickly, and learn from it."

  • Aisle (භාණ්ඩ අතර ඇති පටු මග — Pronounced: "айл") — "Keep the aisle clean."
  • Replenish (නැවත පුරවනවා) — "I replenish empty shelves."
  • Supervisor (පරීක්ෂක / සුපර්වයිසර්) — "I report theft to my supervisor."
