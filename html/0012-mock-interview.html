Lesson 0012 — The Full 30-Question Rapid-Fire Mock Board Rehearsal

Mission link: This is your capstone mastery lesson. On interview day, Israeli representatives and SLBFE officers will ask a rapid sequence of questions across your personal profile, technical skills, Israeli culture, adaptability, and immigration compliance. You have only seconds to respond with clarity, upright posture, and direct camera eye contact. This lesson simulates the entire interview board so you enter the room with absolute confidence and zero anxiety.

Strict Comprehensible Input mode: You will step into the complete interview simulation with Dilan. The lesson is organized into four distinct interview stages with interactive <details> disclosure blocks for instant self-checking. Grammar is placed in the Optional Monitor Appendix.

Personal details used (real): Dilan Harshana, age 28, from Pahalagoda, Tangalle, 5+ years of retail/supermarket experience, forklift qualified, physically strong, 100% prepared to pass.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson — through comprehensive retrieval practice and simulated immersion — you will be able to:
1. Deliver the complete 60-second self-introduction without a single hesitation or time overrun.
2. Answer 30 core interview questions across all 4 course units using crisp, confident SVO sentences.
3. Execute the 17 DOs & DON'Ts evaluation checklist with 100% compliance.
4. Demonstrate unshakeable mental composure under rapid-fire questioning.
5. Hold 2 capstone mastery memory formulas: M.O.C.K.-30 and V.I.C.T.O.R.Y.

Estimated time: 45 minutes, split into 5 comprehensive micro-blocks.
Comprehensible Input level: i + 1 — full integration of all vocabulary, structures, and delivery skills into active spoken fluency.

The Big Idea (read in English — visualize the moment of victory)

The door opens. Dilan performs the K.A.G.S. entry sequence: knocks gently, asks permission, greets the panel, and sits straight. He looks directly into the camera lens with a subtle, confident smile.

For 15 minutes, the Israeli representative fires questions: self-introduction, FIFO stocking, broken bottles, number verification, machine safety, Shabbat, sub-zero freezers, and B1 visa rules.

Dilan does not pause. He does not guess. He uses clear SVO sentences, precise numbers, and respectful composure. At the end, the Israeli director stands up, smiles, and extends his hand:
"Congratulations, Dilan. You did an exceptional interview. Welcome to Israel!"

Mastery is not about luck — it is the result of structured preparation and deliberate practice.

Micro-Block 1 — Stage 1: Entry, Profile & Number Precision (8 min)

CI principle applied: High-intensity active recall of foundational profile details.

Q1: "May I see your document file, and tell me your full name and age?"

Click for Model Answer > "Yes, Sir. Here is my complete document file. > My name is Dilan Harshana. I am twenty-eight years old, and I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka."

Q2: "Deliver your full self-introduction." (Target: Under 60 seconds)

Click for Model Answer (P.E.S.M.S. Framework) > "Good morning, Sir. Thank you for giving me this opportunity. > My name is Dilan Harshana. I am twenty-eight years old, not married, and from Tangalle, Sri Lanka. > I have more than five years of experience in the supermarket and retail sector, including three months of training at Keells Supermarket and more than five years working at a retail supermarket in Tangalle. In addition, I have completed a certified forklift operator course and practical technical training at David Pieris Motors. > In my previous jobs, I was responsible for stocking display shelves, applying the FIFO method, operating pallet jacks, and assisting customers. > I am physically fit, hardworking, and highly reliable. My height is 168 centimeters and my weight is 60 kilograms. > I want to work in an Israeli supermarket to gain valuable international experience, earn a good income in Shekels, and support my family's economy. > That is all about me, Sir. Thank you very much."

Q3: "How much was your previous monthly salary, and what were your weekly work hours?"

Click for Model Answer > "I earned sixty thousand rupees per month, Sir. > I regularly worked fifty hours per week, including overtime."

Micro-Block 2 — Stage 2: Technical Retail & Factory Operations (8 min)

CI principle applied: Technical procedural fluency under pressure.

Q4: "Explain the FIFO method and how you rotate dairy stock."

Click for Model Answer > "FIFO stands for First-In, First-Out, Sir. > When new dairy stock arrives, I check the expiration dates. > I place the newest items at the back of the shelf and pull the older stock to the front to prevent spoilage and store shrink."

Q5: "What immediate steps do you take if an oil bottle breaks in a customer aisle?"

Click for Model Answer (S.P.I.L.L. Protocol) > "Customer safety is my number one priority. > First, I secure the area and place a 'Caution: Wet Floor' warning sign. > Next, wearing PPE gloves, I sweep up broken glass. > Then, I mop the oily floor with chemical degreaser (D10). > I remove the warning sign only after the floor is completely dry."

Q6: "What is PPE, and what will you do if a machine jams on the line?"

Click for Model Answer (S.T.O.P. Protocol) > "PPE stands for Personal Protective Equipment—including safety shoes, helmet, gloves, and goggles. > If a machine jams, I hit the Emergency Stop button immediately, set aside defective goods, and inform my supervisor and maintenance technician right away."

Q7: "Are you prepared for the 50 kg physical stamina test?"

Click for Model Answer (P.T.-50-2-11) > "Yes, Sir! I am physically fit, strong, and healthy. > I am fully prepared to lift a 50 kg load, carry it for 200 meters, and complete the 2 km run in 11 minutes."

Micro-Block 3 — Stage 3: Israel Realities & Cultural Adaptation (8 min)

CI principle applied: Cultural knowledge and psychological resilience.

Q8: "Where is Israel located, what is the capital, and what is the currency?"

Click for Model Answer > "Israel is in the Middle East along the Mediterranean Sea, bordered by Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, and Egypt. > The capital is Jerusalem, the financial hub is Tel Aviv, and the currency is the New Israeli Shekel (NIS)."

Q9: "What is Shabbat, and how does the Israeli workweek run?"

Click for Model Answer > "Shabbat is the Jewish day of rest from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset. > The standard Israeli workweek runs from Sunday to Thursday."

Q10: "How will you handle Israeli supervisors who speak loudly and directly ('Dugri')?"

Click for Model Answer > "I respect the 'Dugri' communication style, Sir. > I do not take direct feedback personally. I stay calm, listen carefully, and execute instructions immediately."

Q11: "Can you work 10-hour night shifts and inside sub-zero (-18°C) freezer rooms?"

Click for Model Answer > "Yes, Sir. I am 100% comfortable with rotating night shifts and overtime. > I wear thermal protective gear and have no problem adapting to extreme climates or cold storage rooms."

Micro-Block 4 — Stage 4: Compliance, Longevity & Immigration (8 min)

CI principle applied: Legal certainty and immigration defense.

Q12: "Under what visa will you work, and what are the legal limits?"

Click for Model Answer (B.1.-V.I.S.A.) > "I will work under a legal B1 work visa, valid for up to 5 years and 3 months. > I am bound to work only for my sponsor employer, and secondary part-time jobs are strictly illegal."

Q13: "What will you do when your 5-year visa expires?"

Click for Model Answer > "I will strictly follow immigration laws and return to Sri Lanka. > I plan to use my savings and international retail experience to start my own supermarket business in Tangalle."

Q14: "At Tel Aviv airport, the immigration officer asks: 'What is the purpose of your visit?' What do you say?"

Click for Model Answer (P.A.S.S. Drill) > "I am here to work under a legal government B1 visa, Sir. Here is my official employment letter and accommodation address."

Micro-Block 2 — Stage 2: Technical Retail & Factory Operations (තාක්ෂණික මෙහෙයුම්) (විනාඩි 8)

Q4: "Explain the FIFO method and how you rotate dairy stock."

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (Model Answer) > "FIFO stands for First-In, First-Out, Sir. > When new dairy stock arrives, I check the expiration dates. > I place the newest items at the back of the shelf and pull the older stock to the front to prevent spoilage and store shrink."

Q5: "What immediate steps do you take if an oil bottle breaks in a customer aisle?"

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (S.P.I.L.L. Protocol) > "Customer safety is my number one priority. > First, I secure the area and place a 'Caution: Wet Floor' warning sign. > Next, wearing PPE gloves, I sweep up broken glass. > Then, I mop the oily floor with chemical degreaser (D10). > I remove the warning sign only after the floor is completely dry."

Q6: "What is PPE, and what will you do if a machine jams on the line?"

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (S.T.O.P. Protocol) > "PPE stands for Personal Protective Equipment—including safety shoes, helmet, gloves, and goggles. > If a machine jams, I hit the Emergency Stop button immediately, set aside defective goods, and inform my supervisor and maintenance technician right away."

Q7: "Are you prepared for the 50 kg physical stamina test?"

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (P.T.-50-2-11) > "Yes, Sir! I am physically fit, strong, and healthy. > I am fully prepared to lift a 50 kg load, carry it for 200 meters, and complete the 2 km run in 11 minutes."

Q9: "What is Shabbat, and how does the Israeli workweek run?"

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (Model Answer) > "Shabbat is the Jewish day of rest from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset. > The standard Israeli workweek runs from Sunday to Thursday."

Q10: "How will you handle Israeli supervisors who speak loudly and directly ('Dugri')?"

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (Model Answer) > "I respect the 'Dugri' communication style, Sir. > I do not take direct feedback personally. I stay calm, listen carefully, and execute instructions immediately."

ආදර්ශ පිළිතුර බැලීමට ක්ලික් කරන්න (Model Answer) > "Yes, Sir. I am 100% comfortable with rotating night shifts and overtime. > I wear thermal protective gear and have no problem adapting to extreme climates or cold storage rooms."

Micro-Block 4 — Stage 4: Compliance, Longevity & Immigration (වීසා සහ ආගමන විගමන) (විනාඩි 8)

  • [ ] Night Before Interview (පෙර දින රාත්‍රියේ): 17 DOs & DON'Ts ලැයිස්තුව සහ ලිපිගොනුව අවසන් වරට පරීක්ෂා කර, පූර්ණ විශ්වාසයෙන් යුතුව විවේක ගන්න.

Lesson 0012 — සම්මුඛ පරීක්ෂණ මණ්ඩල පෙරහුරුව (Mock Board Rehearsal) [කෙටි සාරාංශය සහ මතක සූත්‍ර]

ඉලක්කය: සම්මුඛ පරීක්ෂණයේදී අසන සියලුම ප්‍රධාන ප්‍රශ්නවලට ආත්ම විශ්වාසයෙන්, කෙටි සහ පැහැදිලි පිළිතුරු ලබා දී සාර්ථක වීම.

ප්‍රධාන ඉගෙනුම් ඉලක්ක (Core Goals)

  2. තාක්ෂණික සහ නීතිමය ප්‍රශ්නවලට සරල SVO වාක්‍යවලින් පිළිතුරු දීම.
  3. 17 DOs & DON'Ts ප්‍රමිති අනුගමනය කිරීම.

ප්‍රධාන මතක සූත්‍ර (Memory Keys & Formulas)

1. ජයග්‍රාහී සූත්‍රය: V.I.C.T.O.R.Y.

සම්මුඛ පරීක්ෂණයේදී පවත්වාගත යුතු ප්‍රමිතිය:

අකුර මූලධර්මය
V Visual Eye Contact: කැමරාව/මණ්ඩලය දෙස බැලීම.
I Instant Handover: ලියකියවිලි ක්‍ෂණිකව දීම.
C Clear SVO Sentences: පැහැදිලි සරල වාක්‍ය භාවිතය.
T Technical Precision: FIFO, PPE, S.T.O.P. නිරවද්‍යතාව.
O Open/Confident Posture: කෙළින් ඉරියව්ව.
R Resilient Mindset: පීඩනය හමුවේ සන්සුන් වීම.
Y Yield Legal Compliance: නීතිගරුක බව තහවුරු කිරීම.

ප්‍රායෝගික පුහුණුව (Action Drill)

  2. Review: තත්පර 60 ස්වයං හැඳින්වීම සහ ප්‍රශ්න 14ට දුන් පිළිතුරු පරීක්ෂා කර අඩුපාඩු හදන්න.
  3. Mindset: "මම සූදානම්" යන ආකල්පය තහවුරු කරගෙන සම්මුඛ පරීක්ෂණයට යන්න.

  • Confidence = ආත්ම විශ්වාසය ("I answered all questions with confidence.")
  • Victory = ජයග්‍රහණය ("Preparation leads to victory.")
