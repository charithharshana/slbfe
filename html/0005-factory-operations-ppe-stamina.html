Lesson 0005 - Factory Safety, PPE, And Physical Fitness

Main target: Prove that you work safely, can respond to machine problems, and are ready for physical work.

Remember First

PPE = Personal Protective Equipment: safety shoes, helmet, gloves, goggles, and ear protection.

STOP = Stop - Tell supervisor - Observe and isolate - Prevent restart.

PT-50-2-11: lift 50 kg, carry it 200 m, and run 2 km in 11 minutes. Confirm the current official test requirements before relying on these figures.

Interview Questions And Model Answers

1. “What are your duties as a packing or production worker?”

“My duties include packing finished products, checking quality, keeping my workstation clean, and moving materials when required. I follow safety rules during every task.”

2. “What is PPE, and why is it important?”

“PPE stands for Personal Protective Equipment. It includes safety shoes, a helmet, gloves, goggles, and ear protection. PPE protects workers from injuries, noise, chemicals, and machine hazards.”

3. “What do you do if a machine malfunctions?”

“I stop the machine immediately. I do not try to repair it myself. I set aside defective products and report the problem to my supervisor and the maintenance team.”

4. “Can you work under pressure?”

“Yes, Sir. I stay calm and work systematically under pressure. I meet targets without compromising safety or quality.”

5. “Are you physically fit and healthy?”

“Yes, Sir. I am physically fit and healthy. I have no back pain or physical injury, and I am ready for heavy manual work.”

6. “What packaging materials have you used?”

“I have used cardboard boxes, stretch wrap, bubble wrap, and vacuum packing. I use the correct material to protect each product.”

Comprehensible Input: The Safe Production Line

Before work, Dilan puts on PPE. A machine jams. He presses the emergency stop button, tells his supervisor, separates the defective products, and prevents anyone from restarting the machine. He keeps the workstation clean and continues only when maintenance says it is safe.

Read the story twice. Point to the action as you read it. Then retell it using PPE - STOP - clean - safe.

Pronunciation And Vocabulary

  • PPE: say each letter: “P-P-E”
  • malfunction: mal-FUNK-shun
  • defective: di-FEK-tiv
  • stamina: STAM-i-na
  • protective: pro-TEK-tiv

Practice

  • [ ] Say the physical fitness answer without exaggerating or inventing medical information.
  • [ ] Describe two packaging materials.

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Recite PPE and STOP.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Answer the six questions in random order.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Record the machine-safety and physical-fitness answers.
  • [ ] Before interview: Review all numbers and all safety verbs.

Progress Criterion

Move on when you can give a safe, ordered answer without saying that you will repair dangerous machinery yourself.

Lesson 0005 — Factory Safety, PPE, And Physical Fitness (කර්මාන්තශාලා ආරක්ෂාව, PPE සහ කායික යෝග්‍යතාව)

ප්‍රධාන ඉලක්කය: ආරක්ෂිත සේවා ක්‍රම, PPE භාවිතය, යන්ත්‍ර දෝෂ සඳහා නිවැරදි පියවර සහ කායික යෝග්‍යතාව තහවුරු කිරීම.

1. ආරක්ෂිත ඇඳුම්: PPE සූත්‍රය

  • PPE = Personal Protective Equipment
  • Equipment: Safety shoes, Helmet, Gloves, Goggles, Ear protection
  • Q: "What is PPE, and why is it important?"
  • A: "PPE stands for Personal Protective Equipment. It includes safety shoes, a helmet, gloves, goggles, and ear protection. PPE protects workers from injuries, noise, chemicals, and machine hazards."

2. යන්ත්‍ර දෝෂ සඳහා පියවර: STOP සූත්‍රය

යන්ත්‍රයක් අක්‍රිය වූ විට හෝ දෝෂයක් ආ විට (Machine Malfunction):

අකුර පියවර ක්‍රියාව
S Stop Stop the machine immediately (යන්ත්‍රය වහාම නවත්වන්න)
T Tell supervisor Report to supervisor and maintenance team (තමන් තනිව සදන්න යන්න එපා)
O Observe & isolate Observe and isolate defective products (දෝෂ සහිත නිෂ්පාදන වෙන් කරන්න)
P Prevent restart Prevent restart until authorized (අවසර ලැබෙන තෙක් නැවත ස්ටාර්ට් වීම වළක්වන්න)
  • Q: "What do you do if a machine malfunctions?"
  • A: "I stop the machine immediately. I do not try to repair it myself. I set aside defective products and report the problem to my supervisor and the maintenance team."

3. කායික යෝග්‍යතාව සහ පරීක්ෂණ (Physical Fitness)

  • PT Benchmark (යොමු මානය): 50 kg එසවීම, 200 m රැගෙන යාම, 2 km මිනිත්තු 11න් දිවීම.
  • Q: "Are you physically fit and healthy?"
  • A: "Yes, Sir. I am physically fit and healthy. I have no back pain or physical injury, and I am ready for heavy manual work."
  • Q: "Can you work under pressure?"
  • A: "Yes, Sir. I stay calm and work systematically under pressure. I meet targets without compromising safety or quality."

4. නිෂ්පාදන හා ඇසුරුම් රාජකාරි (Duties & Packaging)

  • Duties: "My duties include packing finished products, checking quality, keeping my workstation clean, and moving materials when required."
  • Packaging Materials: Cardboard boxes, stretch wrap, bubble wrap, vacuum packing.

දෛනික වැදගත් වචන 3 (3 Key Words)

  • Malfunction (යන්ත්‍ර දෝෂ ඇතිවීම) — "Report machine malfunction immediately."
  • Defective (දෝෂ සහිත) — "Set aside defective products."
  • Stamina (කායික දරාගැනීමේ ශක්තිය) — "I have good stamina for long shifts."
