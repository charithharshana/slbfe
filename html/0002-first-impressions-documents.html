Lesson 0002 - First Impression, Documents, And Entry

Main target: Look prepared, hand over documents quickly, and enter the interview professionally.

Remember First

Dress: clean white long-sleeve shirt, black trousers, polished black shoes, short neat hair, clean-shaved face, no strong perfume. Arrive at least 30 minutes early.

Entry sequence: KAGS = Knock - Ask - Greet - Sit.

  1. Knock twice and wait.
  2. “May I come in, Sir?”
  3. “Good morning, Sir.” / “Good afternoon, Sir.”
  4. “May I sit, Sir?” Wait for permission, then say, “Thank you, Sir.”

Interview Questions And Answers

“Can I see your documents?”

“Yes, Sir. Here is my document file.”

“Why are your documents important?”

“They verify my identity, education, work experience, and legal application.”

“What is in your service letter?”

“It shows my company name, full name, NIC number, exact job title, employment period, signature, and official stamp.”

If asked for the file: hand it over immediately. Never search through loose papers.

Comprehensible Input: Interview Morning

Dilan arrives early. His white shirt is clean and his black shoes are polished. His passport, NIC, certificates, service letters, and payslips are in one neat folder. He knocks twice. He asks permission to enter. He greets the panel and waits before sitting. When the panel asks for the file, he hands it over quickly.

Read the scene twice. On the second reading, imagine each action. Then cover the page and retell the scene with early - clean - folder - knock - ask - greet - sit.

Document Checklist

  • Original passport and color copy
  • NIC and color copy
  • Birth certificate and color copy
  • School leaving certificates and copies
  • Service letter or letters
  • At least six payslips when required for experience verification
  • Marriage certificate, if applicable

Verification rule: your spoken answers, CV, service letter, payslips, and previous employer’s answers must match.

Retrieval Check

What does KAGS mean?Knock, Ask permission, Greet, Sit after permission.
What should you wear?A clean white long-sleeve shirt, black trousers, and polished black shoes. Hair should be short and neat; the face should be clean-shaved.
What happens if your spoken experience does not match the verification call?You may be disqualified. Give only truthful, verifiable information.

Physical Practice

  • [ ] Put your documents in the correct folder.
  • [ ] Walk to a door, knock twice, and say the permission phrase.
  • [ ] Enter, greet, ask to sit, and thank the panel.
  • [ ] Repeat the whole KAGS sequence three times.

Pronunciation And Useful Words

  • passport: PASS-port
  • certificate: ser-TIF-i-kit
  • verification: ver-i-fi-KAY-shun
  • original: uh-RIJ-uh-nuhl

Useful words: neat, original, verify. Example: “My original documents are neat and ready for verification.”

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Recite KAGS and the document list.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Perform the entry sequence three times.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Practice a two-second file handover.
  • [ ] Before interview: Check clothing, folder, originals, copies, and arrival time.

Progress Criterion

Move on when entry feels automatic and you can name the document groups without looking.

  • Time Rule: Arrive at least 30 minutes early.

interview ප්‍රශ්න සහ පිළිතුරු (Key Document Q&A)

  • "Can I see your documents?"
  • "Yes, Sir. Here is my document file." (විසිරුණු කොළ අතර නොසොයා වහාම file එක ලබාදෙන්න)
  • "Why are your documents important?"
  • "They verify my identity, education, work experience, and legal application."
  • "What is in your service letter?"
  • "It shows my company name, full name, NIC number, exact job title, employment period, signature, and official stamp."

දෛනික වැදගත් වචන 3 (3 Key Words)

  • Neat (පිළිවෙළ ඇති) — "My document folder is neat."
  • Original (මුල් පිටපත) — "Here are my original certificates."
  • Verify (තහවුරු කරනවා) — "Service letters verify my work experience."
