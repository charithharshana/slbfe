Lesson 0003 - Numbers That Must Be Clear

Main target: Give age, experience, salary, height, weight, and work-hour information accurately.

Remember First

TEEN: stress the end and hold the long “ee”: fif-TEEN, thir-TEEN.

TY: stress the beginning and keep the ending short: FIF-ty, THIR-ty.

memory formula: TEEN = long and strong at the end; TY = short and strong at the start.

Personal Number Answers

Question Model answer
“How old are you?” “I am twenty-eight years old.”
“How many years of experience do you have?” “I have more than five years of experience.”
“How long did you train at Keells?” “I trained at Keells Supermarket for three months.”
“What was your salary?” “I earned sixty thousand rupees per month.”
“What is your height?” “I am one hundred sixty-eight centimeters tall.”
“What is your weight?” “My weight is sixty kilograms.”

Use the exact numbers in your documents. Do not use the sample numbers in this lesson if your records are different.

Comprehensible Input: The Verification Conversation

The interviewer checks Dilan’s documents. “How old are you?” Dilan says, “I am twenty-eight years old.” The interviewer asks about experience. Dilan says, “I have more than five years of experience.” Then the interviewer asks about salary. Dilan says, “I earned sixty thousand rupees per month.” His spoken numbers match his documents.

Read it twice. On the second reading, listen for the numbers. Cover the page and answer the three questions from memory.

Critical Sound Pairs

Teen Ty Interview meaning
thirteen thirty experience or age
fourteen forty salary or quantity
fifteen fifty hours or age
sixteen sixty salary or weight
seventeen seventy weight or quantity
eighteen eighty quantity
nineteen ninety temperature

Pronunciation Drill

Say slowly, then normally:

  • thir-TEEN / THIR-ty
  • fif-TEEN / FIF-ty
  • six-TEEN / SIX-ty
  • eigh-TEEN / EIGH-ty

If you are unsure, repeat the sentence, not only the number: “I earned sixty thousand rupees per month.”

Retrieval Check

Which part is stronger in fifteen?The final syllable: fif-TEEN.
Which part is stronger in fifty?The first syllable: FIF-ty.
Say the four key personal answers.Twenty-eight years old; more than five years of experience; sixty thousand rupees per month; sixty kilograms. Use your verified personal details.

Practice

  1. Listen to a reliable pronunciation source for each pair.
  2. Have a partner say one number. Point to TEEN or TY.
  3. Whisper the six personal answers.
  4. Record them once. Check stress, not accent perfection.

Three Useful Words

  • exact - completely correct
  • salary - money earned from work
  • experience - knowledge gained from work

Sentence: “My exact experience and salary match my documents.”

Sinhala Clarification

Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

  • [ ] Within 1 hour: Say the four pairs aloud.
  • [ ] After 1 day: Answer your six number questions without reading.
  • [ ] After 1 week: Record a document-verification dialogue.
  • [ ] Before interview: Check every age, date, duration, salary, height, and weight against your documents.

Progress Criterion

Move on when a listener can understand your numbers without asking you to repeat them.

  • TY (30, 40, 50, 60...): Short and Strong at the START (ආරම්භය ශක්තිමත් කර අවසානය කෙටි කරන්න)
  • FIF*-ty, THIR-ty, SIX*-ty

සංඛ්‍යාත්මක ප්‍රශ්න සහ පිළිතුරු (Personal Number Answers)

  • Experience (පළපුරුද්ද) — "I have five years of experience."
