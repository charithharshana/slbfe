# Lesson 0002 — First Impressions, Grooming & The Verification File

> **Mission link:** Before you say a single word, the Israeli interviewer observes your appearance, your posture, and how you handle your documents. A disorganized file or mismatched story with your employer's records is an immediate disqualifier (see `01/interview_merged.md` §1.3 and §8.1).

> **Strict Comprehensible Input mode:** This lesson follows Stephen Krashen's Input Hypothesis and the micro-learning principles from `01/info_final.md`. **Meaning in rich context first, zero grammar roadblocks, low anxiety, multiple story encounters, and gentle output at the end.** Explicit grammar remains in the *Optional Monitor Appendix*.

> **Personal details used:** **Dilan Harshana**, age **28**, from **47/1 Pahalagoda, Tangalle**. He holds service verification for his retail supermarket work and Keells experience, payslips, and training certificates from David Pieris Motors Company.

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson — through **comprehensible input, not rote memorization** — you will be able to:
1. **Recognize** the 4 essential interview dress and grooming rules when described in English.
2. **Understand** the document handover drill (*"Give me your file"*) and respond without hesitation.
3. **Know** the exact details Israeli auditors verify when calling past employers.
4. **Acquire** 5 high-frequency documentation terms into your physical 3-Words-a-Day journal.
5. **Whisper and speak** the 4 polite entry and seating phrases naturally.
6. **Retain** the **F.I.L.E.** and **K.A.G.S.** memory anchors for lifetime storage strength.

**Estimated time:** 40 minutes, divided into **5 micro-blocks** (~8 min each) with structured breaks (per `info_final.md` Part IV §6).
**Comprehensible Input level:** *i + 1* — building directly on Lesson 1's greeting foundation.

## Micro-Block 3 — Three Encounters with the Handover (8 min)

> **CI principle applied:** **Repetition with variation.** You experience the exact same interaction through three different angles.

### Encounter 1 — The Room Dialogue (Full Interaction)

*Interviewer:* "Candidate number 14, please come in."  
*Dilan (at the door):* **"May I come in, Sir?"**  
*Interviewer:* "Yes, come in. Please have a seat."  
*Dilan (before sitting):* **"Thank you, Sir. May I sit?"**  
*Interviewer:* "Yes, please. Give me your file."  
*Dilan (holding file with both hands, extending it forward):* **"Here is my file, Sir. All original documents and payslips are inside."**  
*Interviewer:* "Thank you, Dilan. Good morning."  
*Dilan:* **"Good morning, Sir. Thank you for giving me this opportunity."**

### Encounter 2 — The Auditor's Observation Notes

> *"Candidate Dilan Harshana entered the room with polite decorum. He asked permission before entering and sitting. When asked for his documentation, he presented a complete, organized file containing his passport, NIC, school certificates, authenticated service letters, six payslips, and machinery certificates. No time was wasted searching for documents."*

### Encounter 3 — The Simple Memory Rule

> *Knock $\rightarrow$ Ask $\rightarrow$ Greet $\rightarrow$ Sit $\rightarrow$ Hand over.*  
> *"May I come in?" $\rightarrow$ "Good morning, Sir." $\rightarrow$ "May I sit?" $\rightarrow$ "Here is my file, Sir."*

## Micro-Block 4 — Memory Anchors & Mnemonics (6 min)

### 🎵 Mnemonic #1 — The **F.I.L.E.** Checklist
| Letter | Component | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | **Fast Handover** | Hand over immediately without opening or searching. |
| **I** | **Intact Originals** | Passport, NIC, Birth Certificate in pristine condition. |
| **L** | **Legitimate Payslips** | At least 6 payslips showing consistent salary history. |
| **E** | **Exact Dates** | Service letter dates match your spoken answers 100%. |

### ✋ Mnemonic #2 — The **K.A.G.S.** Entry Sequence
Think of the word **KAGS** when you arrive at the interview door:
- **K** — **Knock** gently twice on the door.
- **A** — **Ask**: *"May I come in, Sir / Madam?"*
- **G** — **Greet**: *"Good morning / Good afternoon, Sir."*
- **S** — **Sit**: *"May I sit, Sir?"* $\rightarrow$ Sit upright with hands on knees.

## Optional Monitor Appendix (Grammar in Context)

> **Note:** Do not memorize these rules. They are here only for reference.

* **Modal Verbs for Politeness:**
  * *May I + verb...?* $\rightarrow$ Formal permission (*May I come in?*, *May I sit?*).
  * *Could you + verb...?* $\rightarrow$ Polite request (*Could you please repeat the question?*).
* **Present Perfect for Readiness:**
  * *I have brought my original documents.*
  * *I have prepared six payslips.*
