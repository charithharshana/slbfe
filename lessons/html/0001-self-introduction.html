My First 60 Seconds

Dilan's Story is your opening move. Build a calm, clear self-introduction that fits inside the first minute of an interview.

40 min5 micro-blocks
input → gentle output
01 · Start with meaning

The big idea

8 min

Imagine a bright room in Colombo. Three people sit behind a long table. One panel member smiles and says: “Good morning. Please, tell us about yourself.”

Your next 60 seconds should show five things: who you are, what you have done, what you are good at, why you want the job, and how you finish.

Read and listen before you force yourself to speak. Familiar words become easier when they arrive inside a story you understand.

02 · Context first

Meet Dilan

8 min
ProfileDilan Harshana is 28, not married, has no children, and comes from Pahalagoda, Tangalle, Sri Lanka.
ExperienceHe has more than five years in supermarket and retail work, including three months at Keells Supermarket.
Work styleHe helps customers, checks stock, keeps the shop organized, and is honest, reliable, strong, and on time.
Why Israel? Dilan wants international experience, stronger skills, and a better future. He believes his positive attitude will help the team.
03 · Notice the pattern

The five-part structure

6 min

Use PESMS as a simple sequence. It is like an SMS arriving in the correct order.

PProfileName, age, place, family details
EExperienceJobs, duties, training
SStrengthsHonest, hardworking, reliable, fit
MMotivationWhy Israel and why this work
SSign-offFinish with thanks and confidence

5 fingers Pinky = Profile · Ring = Experience · Middle = Strengths · Index = Motivation · Thumb = Sign-off.

04 · Your model answer

Full 60-second speech

5 min
[PROFILE] Good morning, sir. My name is Dilan Harshana. I am 28 years old, I am not married, and I have no children. I am from Pahalagoda, Tangalle, in Sri Lanka. [EXPERIENCE] I have more than five years of experience in the supermarket and retail field. I worked at Keells Supermarket for three months, and I also ran a retail supermarket in Tangalle. My responsibilities included helping customers, arranging and checking stock, keeping the supermarket clean and organized, and supporting the daily operation of the business. I have also completed a forklift operator course and trained at David Pieris Motors Company, a Bajaj company. [STRENGTHS] I am hardworking, honest, reliable, and physically fit. I always try to arrive on time and complete my work carefully. [MOTIVATION] I want to work in Israel to gain international experience and develop my skills. I am confident that my experience and positive attitude will contribute to your team. [SIGN-OFF] That is all about me. Thank you, sir.

05 · Sound practice

Teen and ty

5 min

-teen ends with a sharper N sound. -ty ends with a shorter, flatter T sound. Listen first, then say each pair.

TeenTySentence
thirteenthirtyI am thirty years old.
fifteenfiftyThe crate weighs fifty kilograms.
fourteenfortyHe worked for forty hours.
eighteeneightyThe box weighs eighty kilos.
06 · Gentle output

Practice without pressure

5 min
1 · ReadRead the speech slowly once. Focus on meaning.
2 · WhisperWhisper it once. Let your voice warm up.
3 · SpeakSay it aloud once. Calm is more important than perfect.

07 · Keep it fresh

1-1-1-1 review plan

5 min
WhenAction
+ 1 hourRead the speech once and whisper your version once.
+ 24 hoursSay it aloud twice and speak three new words five times each.
+ 1 weekTime yourself and finish under 60 seconds.
+ 1 monthRecord a full mock interview and compare your calmness.

Ready when it feels natural
