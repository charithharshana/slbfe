> Dilan reviews the official **Government-to-Government (G2G)** bilateral agreement between Sri Lanka and Israel.  
> Under this program, he will be issued a **B1 Work Visa**.  
> 
> Dilan understands the four strict legal rules:
> 1. **Validity:** The B1 visa is valid for a maximum duration of **5 years and 3 months**.
> 2. **Single Employer:** He is legally bound to work **only** for his allocated sponsor employer.
> 3. **No Side Jobs:** Working part-time or moonlight jobs for another company is **strictly illegal** and causes immediate deportation.
> 4. **Mandatory Return:** When the contract concludes, the worker must return home to Sri Lanka.
> 
> Dilan is law-abiding and disciplined. He respects these regulations 100%.

- **Future Intentions with "Plan to" & "Will":**
  - දිගුකාලීන අභිලාෂයන් සඳහා *"I plan to [verb]"* යොදන්න: *"I plan to start a business."*
  - නීතිමය කැපවීම් ප්‍රකාශ කිරීමට *"I will [verb]"* යොදන්න: *"I will return to Sri Lanka."*
- **Avoid Overstaying Language:** ඊශ්‍රායලය පිළිබඳ කතා කිරීමේදී *"permanently"*, *"forever"*, හෝ *"settle down"* වැනි වචන කිසිවිටෙක නොකියන්න. සැමවිටම *"legal contract period"* යන්න අවධාරණය කරන්න.
