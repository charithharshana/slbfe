# Lesson 0004 - Supermarket Questions: FIFO, Safety, And Customers (සුපර්මාර්කට් ප්‍රශ්න: FIFO, ආරක්ෂාව, සහ පාරිභෝගිකයින්)

> **Main target (ප්‍රධාන ඉලක්කය):** ඔබ retail ක්ෂේත්‍රයේ වැඩ තේරුම් ගෙන ඇති බව තහවුරු කරන ප්‍රායෝගික supermarket ප්‍රශ්නවලට පිළිතුරු දීම.

## Remember First (පළමුව මතක තබා ගන්න)

**FIFO = First-In, First-Out:** පැරණි තොග ඉදිරියට යයි (old stock goes to the front); අලුත් තොග පිටුපසට යයි (new stock goes to the back).

**SPILL = Secure - Place sign - Isolate glass - Liquid mop - Leave clean.** (ආරක්ෂිත කරන්න - සයින් එක තබන්න - වීදුරු වෙන් කරන්න - දියරය පිසදමන්න - පිරිසිදුව තබන්න)

**HEAT = Hear - Empathize - Apologize - Take action.** (සවන්දෙන්න - අනුකම්පාව දක්වන්න - සමාව ඉල්ලන්න - ක්‍රියාමාර්ග ගන්න)

## Interview Questions And Model Answers (සම්මුඛ පරීක්ෂණ ප්‍රශ්න සහ ආදර්ශ පිළිතුරු)

### 1. “What is FIFO, and how do you use it?”

> “FIFO stands for First-In, First-Out. I check the expiration dates. I put new products at the back and move older products to the front. This keeps products fresh and reduces waste.”

### 2. “What do you do if a bottle breaks in a busy aisle?”

> “Customer safety is my first priority. I secure the area and place a warning sign. I wear gloves and remove the broken glass safely. Then I mop the liquid with the correct chemical. I remove the sign only when the floor is dry.”

### 3. “What do you do if a display shelf is empty?”

> “I replenish the shelf immediately. I bring stock from the back storeroom. If the item is out of stock, I inform my supervisor.”

### 4. “How do you help a customer when you cannot understand the language?”

> “I stay calm and polite. I try to understand what the customer needs. If I cannot help, I find a Hebrew-speaking supervisor or manager.”

### 5. “How do you handle a customer complaint?”

> “I listen without interrupting. I show empathy and apologize for the inconvenience. Then I take action or call my supervisor.”

### 6. “Why is chemical safety important?”

> “The correct chemical prevents accidents and keeps the store safe. I wear protective gloves and follow the instructions for each chemical.”

### 7. “How do you work during a long, repetitive shift?”

> “I stay focused and keep a steady pace. I work systematically so the shelves stay full, organized, and accurate.”

### 8. “What do you do if you see theft?”

> “I report it to my supervisor. I do not confront the person myself.”

### 9. “What do you do if you make a mistake?”

> “I report it immediately, fix it quickly, and learn from it.”

## Comprehensible Input: A Morning On The Floor (අවබෝධාත්මක Input: සුපිරි වෙළඳසැල් පරිශ්‍රයේ උදෑසනක්)

> New milk arrives. Dilan checks the dates. He pulls older milk to the front and places new milk at the back. Later, a bottle breaks. He secures the aisle, puts up a warning sign, removes the glass with gloves, and mops the floor. A customer needs help. Dilan listens, stays polite, and finds a supervisor when necessary.

## Key Vocabulary And Pronunciation (ප්‍රධාන වචන මාලාව සහ උච්චාරණය)

- **aisle**: “айл”; **s** අකුර නිහඬය (the s is silent)
- **replenish**: ri-PLEN-ish; නැවත පිරවීම (fill again)
- **expiration date**: කල් ඉකුත්වීමේ දිනය (the date after which food may not be safe)
- **hazard**: අනතුර / අවදානම (danger)
- **supervisor**: පරීක්ෂක / සුපර්වයිසර් (manager responsible for the work)
