# Lesson 0006 - Warehouse, Forklift, And Packing Questions

> **Main target:** Turn your forklift course and stock-handling experience into clear interview English.

## Remember First

**LOAD = Lift safely - Organize stock - Align neatly - Deliver and secure.**

**PACK = Protect goods - Align boxes - Cover with stretch wrap - Keep the load balanced.**

Use exact experience only. Never claim that you operated equipment if you only observed it.

## Interview Questions And Model Answers

### 1. “What experience do you have with warehouse equipment?”

> “I have experience with stock handling and warehouse operations. I have completed a forklift operator course and received practical training at David Pieris Motors Company. I understand safe loading, unloading, pallet movement, and stock organization.”

### 2. “How do you move a heavy pallet safely?”

> “First, I inspect the pallet and the load. Next, I secure the boxes. Then, I insert the forks completely and move at a safe speed. Finally, I place the pallet in the correct storage area.”

### 3. “What do you do when goods arrive?”

> “I check the invoice, count the goods, inspect the boxes, scan the barcodes, and report any damage before storing the stock.”

### 4. “What packaging materials have you used?”

> “I have used cardboard boxes, stretch wrap, bubble wrap, and vacuum packing. I choose the right material to keep the goods secure and undamaged.”

### 5. “What do you do if a delivery box is damaged?”

> “I do not place damaged goods into active stock. I mark the damage, note it on the delivery record, and inform my supervisor.”

## Comprehensible Input: From Dock To Shelf

> A delivery arrives at the loading dock. Dilan checks the invoice and counts the boxes. He inspects the load and scans the barcodes. He secures the pallet with stretch wrap, moves it safely, and places it in the correct storage area. If a box is damaged, he separates it and reports it.

Read it three ways: full story, action verbs only (**check - count - inspect - scan - secure - move - report**), then from memory.

## Essential Vocabulary

- **loading dock** - place where deliveries arrive
- **pallet** - flat base for moving goods
- **pallet jack** - manual tool for moving a pallet
- **forklift** - powered machine for lifting and moving pallets
- **barcode scanner** - tool for recording item codes
- **stretch wrap** - plastic film that secures boxes

Pronunciation: **forklift** = FORK-lift; **pallet** = PAL-it; **invoice** = IN-voys.
