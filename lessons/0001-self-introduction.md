# Lesson 0001 - Your 60-Second Introduction

> **Main target:** Answer “Tell me about yourself” clearly in less than one minute.
> This is the most important answer in the course. Use only facts that match your documents and employer verification.

## Interview Card

**Question:** “Please tell me about yourself.”

**Model answer:**

> “Good morning, Sir. Thank you for giving me this opportunity. My name is Dilan Harshana. I am twenty-eight years old, not married, and I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka. I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work. I trained at Keells Supermarket for three months and worked at a retail supermarket in Tangalle for more than five years. My duties included helping customers, arranging stock, and keeping the supermarket clean and organized. I have also completed a forklift operator course and trained at David Pieris Motors Company. I am hardworking, honest, reliable, and physically fit. I want to work in Israel to gain international experience and develop my skills. That is all about me. Thank you, Sir.”

**Use your real details only.** If a document gives a different job title, date, salary, or period, use the document’s exact information.

## Remember The Shape

**PESMS = Profile - Experience - Strengths - Motivation - Sign-off.**

- **Profile:** name, age, marital status, hometown
- **Experience:** supermarket work, Keells training, retail duties, forklift course
- **Strengths:** hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit
- **Motivation:** international experience and skill development
- **Sign-off:** “That is all about me. Thank you, Sir.”

Think of five shelves. Put one group of information on each shelf. Do not tell your whole life story.

## Comprehensible Input Routine

1. **Understand:** Read the model answer twice. Picture Dilan entering the interview room and speaking to the panel.
2. **Notice:** Read it again and point to the five PESMS shelves. Do not translate every word.
3. **Retrieve:** Hide the answer. Say the five shelves and one fact for each.
4. **Output last:** Read once, whisper once, then speak once. Record only when you feel calm.

### Short Input Story

> Dilan enters the room. He greets the panel. First, he says who he is and where he is from. Next, he explains his supermarket experience and duties. Then, he describes his strengths and training. He explains why he wants to work in Israel. Finally, he closes politely. His answer is short, clear, and professional.

## Essential Follow-Up Questions

| Interviewer asks | Safe answer |
|---|---|
| “What is your name?” | “My name is Dilan Harshana.” |
| “How old are you?” | “I am twenty-eight years old.” |
| “Where are you from?” | “I am from Tangalle, Sri Lanka.” |
| “What is your occupation?” | “I work in supermarket and retail operations.” |
| “How many years of experience do you have?” | “I have more than five years of experience in supermarket and retail work.” |
| “What are your duties?” | “I help customers, arrange stock, and keep the supermarket clean and organized.” |
| “Why do you want this job?” | “I want to gain international experience and develop my skills.” |
| “Why should we hire you?” | “I am hardworking, honest, reliable, physically fit, and ready to learn.” |

## Pronunciation And Delivery

- Speak slowly. Pause after each PESMS section.
- Say **twenty-eight**, not “twenty eight” too quickly.
- Stress the important words: **five years**, **retail**, **forklift**, **reliable**, **Israel**.
- Look at the camera, sit upright, and keep a small natural smile.
- Target: **45-60 seconds**, not longer.

## Practice

1. Read the model answer and underline the five PESMS sections.
2. Close the page and write five cue words: **Profile / Experience / Strengths / Motivation / Sign-off**.
3. Speak from the cue words, not from a full script.
4. Time yourself. Stop before 60 seconds.

## Three Useful Words

- **reliable** - someone people can trust to do the work
- **responsibility** - a duty or task you must do
- **opportunity** - a good chance to do something

Use them in context: “I am reliable.” “My responsibilities included arranging stock.” “Thank you for this opportunity.”

## Review Lock: 1-1-1-1

- [ ] **Within 1 hour:** Say PESMS and the five facts without reading.
- [ ] **After 1 day:** Speak the introduction twice and check the time.
- [ ] **After 1 week:** Answer the eight follow-up questions in random order.
- [ ] **After 1 month / before interview:** Record the full introduction with camera eye contact.

## Progress Criterion

Move on when you can give the introduction in **45-60 seconds**, with accurate personal facts, without reading every sentence.
