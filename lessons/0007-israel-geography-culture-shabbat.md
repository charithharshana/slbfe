# Lesson 0007 — Israel: Geography, Culture, Economy & Shabbat Mastery

> **Mission link:** Israeli representatives always test whether a candidate has genuine knowledge about Israel or is applying blindly. If you do not know the **capital city (Jerusalem)**, the **currency (New Israeli Shekel)**, the **borders**, or what **Shabbat** means, the panel will assume you lack seriousness and cultural adaptability. This lesson ensures you answer all country-specific questions effortlessly.

> **Strict Comprehensible Input mode:** You will explore Israel with Dilan across three narrative scenes: looking at a regional map, exploring the commercial hub of Tel Aviv, and experiencing the weekly rhythm of Shabbat. Comprehension and visualization precede gentle speaking drills. Grammar rules are kept in the Optional Monitor Appendix.

> **Personal details used (real):** **Dilan Harshana**, age **28**, from **Pahalagoda, Tangalle**, preparing for legal overseas employment under the official Israel-Sri Lanka G2G bilateral program.

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson — through **comprehensible input, not mechanical memorization** — you will be able to:
1. **Name Israel's geographic location, sea coast, and 4 border countries** (Lebanon, Syria, Jordan, Egypt).
2. **Identify the capital (Jerusalem), economic hub (Tel Aviv), and currency (New Israeli Shekel / NIS)**.
3. **Explain Israel's economic reputation as the "Startup Nation"** (advanced technology & agriculture).
4. **Describe the Jewish rest day (Shabbat)** and explain the Israeli workweek (Sunday to Thursday).
5. **Hold 2 core cultural mnemonics:** **B.O.R.D.E.R.** and **S.H.A.B.B.A.T.**

**Estimated time:** 40 minutes, split into **5 micro-blocks** of ~8 min each.  
**Comprehensible Input level:** *i + 1* — basic world geography elevated to **concise, factual interview English**.

> **CI principle applied:** Repetition with variation through economic facts.

### 2A. Scene 2 — The "Startup Nation" & The Shekel (5 min)

> Israel is famous worldwide as the **"Startup Nation"**. Despite having a small land area and desert regions, it is a global leader in high-tech innovation, drip irrigation agriculture, water recycling, and modern supermarket logistics.  
> 
> When Dilan works in Israel, he will be paid in the national currency: the **New Israeli Shekel (NIS)**.  
> - 1 Shekel is divided into 100 *Agorot*.  
> - A typical monthly salary for supermarket logistics workers ranges between **7,000 to 8,000 NIS**, which provides a strong financial upgrade to support his family in Sri Lanka.

## Micro-Block 3 — Culture, Faith & Shabbat (8 min)

> **CI principle applied:** High-frequency cultural awareness in realistic workplace context.

### 3A. Scene 3 — The Rhythm of Shabbat (5 min)

> In Israel, the primary religion is **Judaism**, and the official language is **Hebrew**.  
> 
> The Israeli workweek is different from Sri Lanka:
> - The standard workweek runs from **Sunday to Thursday** (or Friday early afternoon).
> - **Shabbat (שַׁבָּת)** is the Jewish holy day of rest.  
> - Shabbat begins at **Friday sunset** and ends at **Saturday sunset**.  
> 
> During Shabbat, public transportation stops, and most government offices and retail stores close so families can rest together. Supermarket and logistics shifts are organized around this weekly cycle.

### 3B. The S.H.A.B.B.A.T. Anchor (3 min)

```

   │   H ──► Holy Day in Judaism                               │
   │   A ──► All Public Offices Close                          │
   │   B ──► Business Resumes Saturday Night                   │
   │   B ──► Better Energy for New Week                        │
   │   A ──► Always Sunday to Thursday Workweek                │

```
