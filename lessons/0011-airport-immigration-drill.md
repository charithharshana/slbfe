# Lesson 0011 — The Ben Gurion Airport Immigration Defense Drill

> **Mission link:** Passing the job interview in Colombo is only step one. When you land at **Ben Gurion International Airport in Tel Aviv**, Israeli border control and customs officers will subject you to a rigorous immigration screening. If you give vague answers, make nervous jokes (e.g., answering *"I want to stay forever"*), or carry undeclared fresh food, you can be detained or deported on the spot. This lesson trains you to pass border control smoothly, calmly, and professionally.

> **Strict Comprehensible Input mode:** Follow Dilan from the moment his flight touches down at Ben Gurion Airport, through baggage claim, and up to the Border Control booth. Comprehension precedes gentle speaking drills. Grammar rules are in the Optional Monitor Appendix.

> **Personal details used (real):** **Dilan Harshana**, age **28**, traveling legally with an official G2G sponsorship letter, B1 work visa, valid passport, and company accommodation contract in Tel Aviv.

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson — through **comprehensible input, not mechanical memorization** — you will be able to:
1. **Answer the 4 critical immigration officer questions** (Purpose, Accommodation, Duration, Customs Declaration) with zero hesitation.
2. **Avoid the fatal jokes and vague replies** that trigger border detention.
3. **Present official travel documents within 2 seconds** at the border control counter.
4. **Follow international customs rules regarding undeclared agriculture/fresh food**.
5. **Hold 2 core border defense mnemonics:** **P.A.S.S.** and **B.O.R.D.E.R.**

**Estimated time:** 40 minutes, split into **5 micro-blocks** of ~8 min each.  
**Comprehensible Input level:** *i + 1* — high-stakes immigration English formatted into **foolproof, direct SVO answers**.

## The Big Idea (read in English — visualize the airport terminal)

> *Dilan walks down the brightly lit arrival corridor of Ben Gurion Airport. He reaches the Border Control booth. A serious Israeli border officer in blue uniform looks at him and asks with a cold, firm voice:*  
> **"What is the purpose of your visit to Israel?"**  
>
> *Dilan does not stutter or say "I want to see the country." He smiles gently, hands over his passport, and replies directly:*  
> **"I am here to work under a legal government B1 work visa, Sir. Here is my official employment letter."**  
>
> *The officer checks the documents, stamps the entry slip, and hands it back: "Welcome to Israel. Next!"*

At airport immigration, **short, factual, documented answers** guarantee a fast, smooth entry.

## Micro-Block 2 — The 4 Border Question Blueprints (8 min)

> **CI principle applied:** High-contrast comparison between dangerous mistakes and correct answers.

### 2A. Scene 2 — The Border Control Counter (5 min)

#### Question 1: Purpose of Visit
- ❌ **Fatal Mistake:** *"I am here for fun / I want to travel around."* (Triggers suspicion of illegal tourist labor)
- ✅ **Correct Answer:** **"I am here to work under a legal government B1 visa, Sir."**

#### Question 2: Where will you stay?
- ❌ **Fatal Mistake:** *"I don't know, my agency knows."* (Shows complete unpreparedness)
- ✅ **Correct Answer:** **"I will stay at the employee accommodation provided by my company. Here is my official employment letter showing the address."**

#### Question 3: How long will you stay in Israel?
- ❌ **Fatal Mistake:** *"Forever!"* (Even as a joke, this causes immediate detention and investigation)
- ✅ **Correct Answer:** **"I will stay for the duration of my legal work visa, Sir."**

#### Question 4: Do you have anything to declare?
- ❌ **Fatal Mistake:** Bringing undeclared fresh fruits, plants, or meat in your luggage (leads to heavy fines and confiscation).
- ✅ **Correct Answer:** **"No, I have nothing to declare, Sir."**  
  *(If carrying sealed commercial Sri Lankan tea/spices, say: "Yes, I have some packaged dry food items to declare.")*

### 2B. The P.A.S.S. Border Defense Mnemonic (3 min)

| Letter | Action | Professional Border Reply |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **P** | **Purpose = Work** | *"I am here to work under a legal B1 visa."* |
| **A** | **Address Ready** | *"I stay at company housing; here is the letter."* |
| **S** | **Stay = Visa Time**| *"I will stay for the duration of my legal visa."* |
| **S** | **Screen Declaration**| *"I have nothing to declare / only packaged goods."* |

> **මතක තබා ගැනීමේ කෙටි ක්‍රමය:** **P.A.S.S.** = Purpose (රැකියාව), Address (සමාගමේ නවාතැන), Stay (වීසා කාලය පමණි), Screen (තහනම් ද්‍රව්‍ය නැති බව ප්‍රකාශ කිරීම).

## Micro-Block 3 — Customs & Agriculture Protection (8 min)

> **CI principle applied:** Repetition with variation through customs compliance rules.

### 3A. Scene 3 — Baggage Claim & Green/Red Customs Channel (5 min)

> After passing the passport booth, Dilan collects his checked luggage from the baggage carousel.  
> He walks toward the exit channels:
> - **Green Channel:** "Nothing to Declare" (No illegal goods, no fresh agricultural items).
> - **Red Channel:** "Goods to Declare" (Carrying commercial items or declared goods).
> 
> Israel has strict biosecurity laws to protect its modern agriculture. **It is strictly forbidden to bring fresh fruit, raw vegetables, seeds, or unsealed meat.**  
> Dilan packed only sealed, commercially packaged tea and dry spices. He walks calmly through customs with zero violations.

## Optional Monitor Appendix (For Grammar Enthusiasts Only)
> *Note: Language acquisition happens naturally through understanding messages. Grammar rules below are for reference only.*

- **Directness with Authority:** Immigration officers do not want small talk. Keep answers to **one single sentence** containing the exact fact requested.
- **Document Handover Etiquette:** Always hold your passport and visa letter in your right hand, ready to hand over the second you step up to the counter.
