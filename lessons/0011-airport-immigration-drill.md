# Lesson 0011 - Airport Immigration Answers

> **Main target:** Give short, factual answers at Ben Gurion Airport and show the correct documents.

## Remember First

**PASS = Purpose - Address - Stay - Screen declaration.**

Keep your passport, B1 visa, employment letter, accommodation address, and G2G documents ready. Answer only the question asked.

## The Four Questions To Memorize

### 1. “What is the purpose of your visit?”

> “I am here to work under a legal government B1 visa. Here is my employment letter.”

Do not say “I am here for fun” or “I want to stay forever.”

### 2. “Where will you stay?”

> “I will stay at the employee accommodation provided by my company. Here is the official address.”

### 3. “How long will you stay?”

> “I will stay for the duration of my legal work visa.”

### 4. “Do you have anything to declare?”

> “No, Sir. I have nothing to declare.”

If you carry permitted packaged food, say exactly what it is and declare it. Never hide fresh fruit, plants, raw vegetables, or other restricted items.

## Comprehensible Input: At The Border

> Dilan reaches the border counter with his passport and work documents. The officer asks four questions. Dilan answers about legal work, company accommodation, the visa period, and customs. His answers are short and match his papers. He stays calm and does not make jokes.

Read the story twice. Then cover it and recall **work - address - visa - declaration**.

- [ ] Answer in one or two factual sentences.

Move on when you can answer all four questions without vague wording, jokes, or document searching.
