# Lesson 0010 — Visa Regulations, Contract Longevity & Future Plans

> **Mission link:** Recruiters want to ensure that candidates will work faithfully for the full 5-year contract, respect Israeli immigration laws, and return to Sri Lanka legally when the visa expires. If you mention illegal part-time work, show uncertainty about staying 5 years, or fail to state a clear future plan, your visa sponsorship will be canceled immediately. This lesson teaches you how to articulate 100% legal compliance and long-term ambition.

> **Strict Comprehensible Input mode:** Follow Dilan across three narrative scenes: understanding his B1 visa contract, demonstrating loyalty on the job, and sharing his 5-year entrepreneurial vision upon returning to Sri Lanka. Comprehension precedes gentle speaking drills. Grammar rules are in the Optional Monitor Appendix.

> **Personal details used (real):** **Dilan Harshana**, age **28**, from **Pahalagoda, Tangalle**, single, disciplined, planning to save his earnings in New Israeli Shekels to build his own modern retail business back in Tangalle.

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson — through **comprehensible input, not mechanical memorization** — you will be able to:
1. **Explain the rules of the B1 work visa** (5 years 3 months maximum, single sponsor employer, no illegal side jobs).
2. **State 100% commitment to completing the full 5-year contract** with high loyalty.
3. **Articulate your return plan and future business ambition** upon completing your legal stay in Israel.
4. **Demonstrate understanding of legal immigration compliance** (zero overstaying).
5. **Hold 2 core compliance mnemonics:** **B.1.-V.I.S.A.** and **P.L.A.N.**

**Estimated time:** 40 minutes, split into **5 micro-blocks** of ~8 min each.  
**Comprehensible Input level:** *i + 1* — legal and career concepts expressed in **crystal-clear, persuasive SVO English**.

## Micro-Block 1 — Pure Immersion: The B1 Visa Rules (8 min)

> **CI principle applied:** Pre-input immersion through legal clarity ($i+1$).

### 1A. Scene 1 — The B1 Work Visa Contract (4 min)

Read this story slowly. Understand the legal framework:

> Dilan reviews the official **Government-to-Government (G2G)** bilateral agreement between Sri Lanka and Israel.  
> Under this program, he will be issued a **B1 Work Visa**.  
> 
> Dilan understands the four strict legal rules:
> 1. **Validity:** The B1 visa is valid for a maximum duration of **5 years and 3 months**.
> 2. **Single Employer:** He is legally bound to work **only** for his allocated sponsor employer.
> 3. **No Side Jobs:** Working part-time or moonlight jobs for another company is **strictly illegal** and causes immediate deportation.
> 4. **Mandatory Return:** When the contract concludes, the worker must return home to Sri Lanka.
> 
> Dilan is law-abiding and disciplined. He respects these regulations 100%.

### 1B. The B.1.-V.I.S.A. Legal Mnemonic (4 min)

| Letter | Rule | Professional Interview Sentence |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **B** | **Bound to 1 Sponsor** | *"I will work only for my allocated sponsor company."* |
| **V** | **Valid for 5 Years** | *"My visa is valid for up to 5 years and 3 months."* |
| **I** | **Illegal Side Jobs Banned** | *"I will never engage in unauthorized part-time work."* |
| **S** | **Save Capital** | *"I will save my earnings systematically in Shekels."* |
| **A** | **Always Return Legally** | *"I will return to Sri Lanka when my visa expires."* |

## Optional Monitor Appendix (For Grammar Enthusiasts Only)
> *Note: Language acquisition happens naturally through understanding messages. Grammar rules below are for reference only.*

- **Future Intentions with "Plan to" & "Will":**
  - Use *"I plan to [verb]"* for long-term career goals: *"I plan to start a business."*
  - Use *"I will [verb]"* for firm legal commitments: *"I will return to Sri Lanka."*
- **Avoid Overstaying Language:** Never use words like *"permanently"*, *"forever"*, or *"settle down"* when discussing Israel. Always emphasize *"legal contract period"*.
