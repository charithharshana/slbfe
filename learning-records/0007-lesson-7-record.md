## Key Learning Points

### 1. Mandatory Geographic Facts
Candidates are expected to instantly name:
- **Capital:** Jerusalem
- **Main Commercial City:** Tel Aviv
- **Surrounding Borders:** Lebanon (North), Syria (Northeast), Jordan (East), Egypt (Southwest)
- **Sea Coast:** Mediterranean Sea

### 2. Economic Identity & Currency
- Israel is known as the **"Startup Nation"** due to high-tech and advanced agricultural innovation.
- The official currency is the **New Israeli Shekel (NIS)**.

### 3. Cultural & Workweek Rhythm
- Official language is **Hebrew**; primary religion is **Judaism**.
- Workweek runs **Sunday to Thursday**.
- **Shabbat** runs from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset (stores/transport close).
