## Key Learning Points

### 1. Technical FIFO Execution
Israeli recruiters test if candidates know real supermarket operations or just general words:
- **FIFO Principle:** Older stock moves to the front; new delivery placed at the back.
- **Goal:** Minimize store waste (*shrink*), prevent expired products on display, guarantee fresh merchandise for shoppers.

### 2. The S.P.I.L.L. Safety Protocol
When glass/liquid breaks on the retail floor:
1. **S**ecure area (warn customers)
2. **P**lace warning sign ("Caution Wet Floor")
3. **I**solate glass & sweep using protective gloves (PPE)
4. **L**iquid mop with chemical degreaser (D10)
5. **L**eave clean & remove cone only when 100% dry

### 3. Customer Interaction Under Language Barrier
- Applying **H.E.A.T.** (Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Take Action) prevents panic when encountering Hebrew-speaking customers.
- Guiding customers physically or locating a bilingual supervisor is the standard operational protocol.
