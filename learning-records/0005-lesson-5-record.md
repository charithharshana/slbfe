## Key Learning Points

### 1. The Safety-First Principle
In Israeli manufacturing, safety is non-negotiable. Taking shortcuts or attempting amateur machine repairs leads to disqualification.
- When machines malfunction, the only acceptable response is hitting the emergency stop button and informing maintenance/supervisor immediately.

### 2. PPE Compliance
Candidates must effortlessly list core PPE items:
- Steel-toe boots, hard hat (helmet), safety goggles, earplugs, protective gloves.

### 3. The G2G Physical Stamina Test Standards
- **Lifting:** 50 kg crate/sack lifted safely to chest height using legs.
- **Carrying:** 200 meters continuous loaded carry.
- **Running:** 2 km endurance run completed within 11 minutes.
