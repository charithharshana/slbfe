## Key Learning Points

### 1. The 5-Second Rule
Interviewers form their first impression within **5 seconds** based purely on visual cues (dress code, grooming, posture). This is before any speaking begins.

### 2. Mandatory G2G Dress Code
- Clean **white long-sleeve shirt**
- **Black trousers**
- **Polished black shoes**
- Hair **short and neat**
- **Clean-shaved** face
- No strong perfumes
- Arrive **30 minutes early**

### 3. The 7 Mandatory Documents
1. Passport (original + color copy)
2. NIC (original + color copy)
3. Birth certificate (original + color copy)
4. School leaving certificates (original + copies)
5. Service letter(s) with company stamp and HR/Store Manager signature
6. Minimum 6 payslips spread across employment timeline
7. Marriage certificate (if applicable)

### 4. The Service Letter Verification Trap
**Critical insight:** Israeli representatives and SLBFE officers **call previous employers** to verify service letters. They ask about:
- Job duties
- Salary history
- Employment duration
- Work ethic

**If interview answers do not match the manager's phone verification = immediate disqualification.**

Service letters must include:
- Company name (official letterhead)
- Employee full name (matching passport/NIC exactly)
- NIC number
- Exact job title
- Exact employment duration (start date to end date)
- Company official stamp/seal
- HR Manager or Store Manager signature with printed name and title

### 5. The K.A.G.S. Entry Ritual
Professional entry sequence:
1. **K**nock twice gently (tap-tap)
2. **A**sk permission: *"May I come in, Sir?"*
3. **G**reet: *"Good morning/afternoon/evening, Sir"*
4. **S**it only after asking: *"May I sit, Sir?"* and receiving permission

### 6. The F.I.L.E. Document Organization System
- **F**ast Handover: Can hand over file instantly when requested
- **I**ntact Originals: No torn, crumpled, or stained documents
- **L**egitimate Payslips: Minimum 6 payslips to verify salary history
- **E**xact Dates: Service letter dates match interview answers exactly

### 7. The 2-Second Test
Recruiters mentally time how long it takes a candidate to hand over their document file. Instant handover = organized, professional. Searching through papers for 20+ seconds = disorganized, unprofessional.
