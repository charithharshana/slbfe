## Key Learning Points

### 1. Legal Integrity
The B1 visa allows work exclusively for the allocated employer for up to 5 years and 3 months. Secondary work or visa overstaying leads to immediate deportation and legal penalties.

### 2. Longevity Commitment
Recruiters look for high retention probability. Candidates must confirm without hesitation that they will remain loyal to one employer for the full 5-year period.

### 3. Purpose-Driven Return Plan
Candidates with clear entrepreneurial goals (saving money to open a business in Sri Lanka) are rated highest because they demonstrate discipline and zero intention to remain illegally.
