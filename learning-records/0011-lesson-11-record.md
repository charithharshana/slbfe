## Key Learning Points

### 1. Border Control Precision
Border control officers look for clarity and consistency. Answers must strictly match the B1 visa and employment contract details.
- Purpose: "To work under a legal government B1 visa."
- Address: "Company accommodation in Tel Aviv (letter provided)."
- Duration: "Duration of the legal work visa."

### 2. Zero-Tolerance Customs Biosecurity
Carrying undeclared fresh fruits, plants, seeds, or unsealed meats can result in immediate fines and entry cancellation. Only commercially packaged goods should be carried and declared if required.
