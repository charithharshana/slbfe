## Key Learning Points

### 1. The Toughness Factor
Israeli recruiters filter for candidates who are mentally resilient and physically enduring. Answers to shift work, temperature, and war questions must be immediate, positive, and free of complaints or emotional sob stories.

### 2. Temperature Readiness
- Working in sub-zero freezers (-18°C) is routine in retail logistics; candidates must state they wear thermal PPE and have no medical issues.

### 3. Living in Shared Accommodation
- Showing respect for different cultures, maintaining room cleanliness, and cooperating with coworkers from other countries demonstrates social maturity.
