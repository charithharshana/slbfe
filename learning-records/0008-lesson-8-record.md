## Key Learning Points

### 1. The "Dugri" Mindset
Israeli work culture prizes directness, speed, and unfiltered honesty. Direct criticism is about the task, not personal insult. Candidates who smile, remain calm, and fix problems immediately are rated as highly resilient.

### 2. The Universal Clarification Tool
Never stay silent or guess when an interview question is missed. The gold formula:
👉 *"Pardon me, Sir. Could you please repeat the question?"*

### 3. Handling Mistakes
Taking direct responsibility without blaming others or providing long excuses demonstrates professional maturity.
