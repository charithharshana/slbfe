## Key Learning Points

### 1. Articulating Heavy Machinery & Logistics Training
Dilan's forklift operator qualification and David Pieris training are high-value differentiators. The lesson gives him direct English sentences to describe operating equipment safely within designated weight limits and following clear visual alerts.

### 2. Standard Receiving Protocol
Receiving stock is a 4-step sequence:
1. Check delivery count against invoice.
2. Inspect for box or moisture damage.
3. Scan barcodes into inventory system.
4. Transport and store using pallet jack or forklift.

### 3. Packaging Terminology
Candidates must effortlessly differentiate and name packaging methods (stretch wrap, bubble wrap, vacuum packing, rigid cardboard corners) to demonstrate real industrial experience.
