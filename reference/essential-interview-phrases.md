# Essential Interview Phrases - Quick Reference

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-19  
**Purpose:** Memorize these phrases for automatic use in interviews

---

## Entry & Permission (Mandatory)

| Situation | Exact Phrase |
|-----------|--------------|
| Before entering room | **"May I come in, Sir/Madam?"** |
| Before sitting down | **"May I sit, Sir?"** |
| After sitting | **"Thank you very much for giving me this opportunity."** |

---

## Time-Based Greetings

| Time | Greeting |
|------|----------|
| 6:00 AM - 11:59 AM | **"Good morning, Sir/Madam."** |
| 12:00 PM - 5:59 PM | **"Good afternoon, Sir/Madam."** |
| 6:00 PM onwards | **"Good evening, Sir/Madam."** |

---

## Recovery Phrases (When You Don't Understand)

| Situation | Phrase |
|-----------|--------|
| Didn't hear the question | **"Pardon me, Sir, could you please repeat the question?"** |
| Need repetition | **"Sorry, Sir, I did not hear clearly. Could you say that again?"** |
| Need thinking time | **"That's a good question, Sir. May I take a moment to answer?"** |

---

## Closing Phrases

| Situation | Phrase |
|-----------|--------|
| End of self-introduction | **"That is all about me, thank you, Sir."** |
| End of interview | **"Thank you very much for your time, Sir."** |
| Leaving the room | **"Have a good day, Sir. Goodbye."** |

## Common Interview Openers (Your Responses)

| Interviewer Says | You Respond |
|------------------|-------------|
| "Tell me about yourself" | Use 8-Step Self-Introduction (See Lesson 2) |
| "Why do you want this job?" | **"I am hardworking and fit for this work..."** |
| "Do you have experience?" | **"I have [X] years experience in..."** |
| "Can you work long hours?" | **"Yes, I am completely flexible and comfortable..."** |
| "Any questions for us?" | **"When will I know the result?"** or **"No, thank you. Everything is clear."** |

## Body Language Reminders

✓ **DO:**
- Stand/sit straight (good posture)
- Make eye contact with interviewer
- Smile subtly (not too much, not serious)
- Keep hands on lap or table (not in pockets)
- Nod slightly when listening
- Speak slowly and clearly

✗ **DON'T:**
- Slouch or lean back
- Look at floor or ceiling
- Touch your face/hair
- Cross arms
- Fidget with hands
- Rush your words
- Look at watch/phone

## Numbers Pronunciation Guide

| Number | Correct | Common Mistake |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| 13 | thir-TEEN | "thirty" |
| 30 | THIR-ty | "thirteen" |
| 15 | fif-TEEN | "fifty" |
| 50 | FIF-ty | "fifteen" |
| 60 | SIX-ty | "sixteen" |
| 70 | SEVEN-ty | "seventeen" |
| 80 | EIGH-ty | "eighteen" |
| 90 | NINE-ty | "nineteen" |

**Rule:** -TEEN = stress on TEEN (higher pitch)  
**Rule:** -TY = stress on first syllable (lower pitch)

## Critical "DON'T SAY" List

❌ **NEVER say these in interview:**
- "I don't know" (say: "I am not sure, but I believe...")
- "I can't do that" (say: "I am willing to learn how to do that")
- "My English is not good" (just speak—don't apologize)
- "I really need this job badly" (too desperate—say: "I am very interested in this opportunity")
- "Can I...?" (use "May I...?" instead)
- "Yeah" / "Yep" (say "Yes, Sir")
- "Nope" / "Nah" (say "No, Sir")

## Professional Vocabulary (Use These)

**Instead of casual words, use professional words:**

| Casual | Professional |
|--------|--------------|
| "boss" | supervisor / manager |
| "job" | position / role |
| "work" | duties / responsibilities |
| "money" | salary / income |
| "really good" | excellent / highly capable |
| "do stuff" | perform tasks / handle duties |
| "help people" | assist customers / provide support |
